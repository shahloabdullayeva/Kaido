# HVAC and APU issues
Air conditioning, heat, blowers and auxiliary power units.
::: issue No AC (blows warm) | soon
looks: Vents blow but not cold. AC compressor clutch not spinning, or spinning and cycling on and off fast.
why: Low refrigerant from a leak (oily, dirty spot at a fitting, hose or condenser is the tell), compressor clutch, pressure switch, blend door, clogged condenser in front of the radiator.
do: Leak test and recharge ($200-500), find the leak (dye). Compressor $1,200-2,500 installed.
:::
::: issue AC compressor noisy | soon
hear: Grinding or rattling from the compressor when AC is on.
do: Turn off AC until checked. A seized compressor can throw the belt.
:::
::: issue Blower not working | soon
looks: No air from vents on any speed, or only on high.
why: Blower motor, blower resistor (works only on high), fuse, switch.
do: Resistor $50-150; motor $200-500.
:::
::: issue No heat | soon
looks: Cold air only even when the engine is warm.
why: Low coolant, thermostat stuck open, heater valve, blend door, plugged heater core.
do: Check coolant first.
:::
::: issue Heater core leaking | soon
looks: Wet carpet on the passenger side, fog on the inside of the windshield, sticky film on glass.
smell: Sweet coolant smell in the cab.
do: Replace core ($800-2,000 because the dash often comes apart).
:::
::: issue Water in the sleeper or cab | watch
looks: Wet floor or bunk after rain, water dripping from the roof, AC drain clogged.
why: Clogged AC condensate drain, roof seals, windshield seal.
do: Clear the drain, reseal.
:::
::: issue APU will not start | soon
looks: APU control panel shows a fault, or nothing.
why: APU fuel filter, APU battery, belt, low coolant, overdue service, glow plug.
do: Check panel code, APU fuel and battery. APU service every 1,000-1,500 hours.
:::
::: issue APU running but no AC or heat | soon
looks: APU engine runs but the sleeper stays hot or cold.
why: APU belt, AC charge on the APU loop, heater fuel pump.
do: Shop with APU dealer (Thermo King TriPac, Carrier ComfortPro).
:::
::: issue Bunk heater (Espar, Webasto) fails | soon
looks: Heater tries, then shuts off; white smoke from its exhaust; error code on the controller.
why: Low fuel, clogged fuel pump, carbon in the burner, low batteries.
do: Service the heater ($200-600). Keep tanks over 1/4 in winter: the heater pickup is higher than the engine's.
:::
