# Electrical and starting issues
Batteries, alternators, cables, grounds, wiring and modules.
::: issue Batteries dead | soon
looks: Nothing on the dash, or dash dims and clicks when cranking.
why: Left on lights or inverter, APU not charging, a battery failed and drained the others, parasitic drain from aftermarket gear, cold weather.
check: Voltage per battery with the cables off. Load test.
do: Jump. Replace all batteries in the bank together if they are over 3-4 years old or one is bad.
cost: $180-300 per group 31 battery; most trucks have 3-4.
:::
::: issue Corroded battery terminals or cables | soon
looks: White, blue or green fuzzy powder on terminals; cables swollen or green under the insulation; terminal loose by hand.
do: Clean, replace cable ends or cables ($100-500).
:::
::: issue Battery box or hold-down broken | watch
looks: Batteries move in the box, broken hold-down, box cracked. Rub marks on cables.
dot: Batteries not secured is a violation.
do: Fix hold-down ($50-200).
:::
::: issue Alternator not charging | soon
dash: Battery or charging lamp, voltmeter under 13 V while running, Kaido alert "not charging".
looks: Lights dim at idle, dash goes crazy as voltage drops.
why: Alternator, belt, broken charge wire, blown fuse link.
do: Shop before the batteries die. Alternator $500-1,200 installed.
:::
::: issue Overcharging | soon
dash: Voltage over 14.8 V.
looks: Batteries hot, swelling, acid smell (rotten eggs), bulbs burning out often.
do: Shop: voltage regulator or alternator. Overcharging ruins batteries and modules.
:::
::: issue Starter failing | soon
hear: Single loud click with good batteries; slow crank; grinding (starter gear not engaging the flywheel); starter keeps spinning after the engine starts.
do: Replace starter ($500-1,200 installed). Grinding can damage the flywheel ring gear.
:::
::: issue Bad ground | soon
looks: Random electrical problems that come and go: gauges jumping, lights flickering, modules resetting, many unrelated codes at once.
why: Corroded or loose ground straps between engine, frame and cab.
do: Clean and tighten grounds ($100-300). Often the cheapest fix for weird electrical trouble.
:::
::: issue Chafed or shorted wiring | soon
looks: Wire insulation worn through where it rubs a bracket or frame, melted wiring, blown fuses that keep blowing.
smell: Burning plastic.
do: Shop. Fix the harness and add protection. A short near fuel lines is a fire risk.
:::
::: issue Data link (CAN) fault | soon
dash: Many lamps on at once, gauges dropping to zero, "no communication" messages, transmission stuck in one gear.
why: J1939 network wiring damaged, terminating resistor, water in a connector, a module pulling the network down.
do: Shop with a laptop. Diagnosis takes time, budget 2-4 hours labor.
:::
::: issue Blown fuse or relay | watch
looks: One item dead (lights on one side, wipers, horn, power window).
do: Check the fuse panel under the dash or in the battery box. Carry spares.
:::
::: issue Inverter or APU drain | watch
looks: Batteries dead after a weekend parked with a fridge or inverter connected.
do: Low-voltage disconnect, or turn off the inverter. Separate APU batteries help.
:::
::: issue ELD or camera wiring loose | watch
looks: Samsara shows the truck not reporting, camera offline, Kaido "ELD not reporting" alert. Driver sees the gateway light off.
why: Gateway unplugged at the diagnostic port, loose power cable, blown fuse on the camera.
do: Ask the driver to check the cable at the 9-pin port under the dash. Re-seat. Samsara support can see the last heartbeat.
:::
