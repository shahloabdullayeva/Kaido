# Frame, fifth wheel and coupling
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frame rails | Two long steel beams | Cracks, rust, bends (can mean out of service) | Weld repair $500 to $3,000; rail replacement much more |
| Crossmembers | Join the frame rails | Cracks, loose bolts | $400 to $1,500 |
| Suspension mounts | Brackets for suspension parts | Cracks | $300 to $1,000 |
| Cab mounts | Rubber mounts under the cab | Cab shake, noise | $200 to $600 each |
| Fifth wheel | Plate that locks the trailer | Won't lock, worn jaws | Rebuild kit $300 to $800; new $1,500 to $3,500 |
| Fifth wheel locking jaws and release handle | Grip the kingpin | Won't release or won't lock (dangerous) | $200 to $600 |
| Fifth wheel slide (sliding plate) | Moves the fifth wheel | Stuck, air cylinder leak | $300 to $800 |
| Fifth wheel mount | Bolts fifth wheel to the frame | Loose bolts, cracks | $300 to $1,000 |
| Deck plate / catwalk | Platform behind the cab | Broken, loose | $200 to $600 |
| Headache rack / back of cab rack | Behind the cab, holds hoses | Damage | $500 to $1,500 |
| Tow hooks | Front of the truck | Bent | $100 to $300 |
| Pintle hook (some trucks) | Rear hitch for other trailers | Wear | $150 to $400 |
