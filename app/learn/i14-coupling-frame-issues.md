# Fifth wheel, coupling and frame issues
Fifth wheels, kingpins, gladhands, 7-way cords, frames and crossmembers.
::: issue Fifth wheel not locked | stop
looks: Jaws not closed around the trailer kingpin, release handle out, gap between the fifth wheel plate and the trailer apron. The lock indicator (on many models) does not show locked.
check: Tug test (pull against the trailer with its brakes on), then look under with a flashlight: jaws closed behind the kingpin, handle in.
do: Never drive until it is locked. Dropped trailers come from this.
:::
::: issue Fifth wheel worn or loose | soon
looks: Too much slack: trailer clunks forward and back when braking and starting. Cracked plate, broken mounting bolts, worn bracket pins.
dot: Loose mounting or cracked plate is out of service.
do: Adjust lock (many fifth wheels have an adjustment), rebuild jaws ($300-800), or replace ($2,000-4,000).
:::
::: issue Fifth wheel dry | watch
looks: Plate shiny and dry or rusty, no grease. Trailer groans when turning.
do: Grease the plate (or use a lube plate). Dry plates wear and make steering heavy.
:::
::: issue Slider on fifth wheel not locked | soon
looks: Sliding fifth wheel locking pins not fully in the holes.
do: Lock it. A loose slider shifts on braking.
:::
::: issue 7-way cord damaged | soon
looks: Cord jacket cut, cord stretched, plug pins corroded green, plug cracked.
dash: Trailer ABS lamp, trailer lights flicker or out.
do: Replace cord ($60-150) or clean plug. Use dielectric grease.
:::
::: issue Frame crack | stop
looks: Crack in the frame rail, usually near a crossmember, spring hanger or fifth wheel mount, rust line or fresh paint crack, sagging frame.
dot: Out of service in most cases.
do: Shop. Welding frames needs a qualified frame shop.
:::
::: issue Crossmember broken or loose | soon
looks: Crossmember cracked, rivets or bolts missing or loose.
do: Repair.
:::
::: issue Mud flaps or brackets missing | watch
looks: Mud flap torn off, bracket bent.
do: Replace ($50-200). Some states require them.
:::
::: issue Deck plate or catwalk damaged | watch
looks: Deck plate behind the cab cracked or loose, steps broken.
do: Repair for driver safety.
:::
