# Normal readings
The numbers a healthy truck shows, so you know when a reading is wrong.
## Engine
| Reading | Normal | Warning | Danger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oil pressure at idle, warm | 15-30 psi | Under 15 psi | Under 10 psi, red lamp |
| Oil pressure at cruise | 35-65 psi | Under 30 psi | Under 20 psi |
| Coolant temperature | 185-210 °F | Over 215 °F | Over 225-230 °F, stop lamp |
| Intake manifold temperature | 90-140 °F | Over 160 °F | Over 180 °F |
| Boost at full load | 25-45 psi (depends on engine) | Low boost with black smoke | - |
| Engine speed at idle | 600-700 rpm | Hunting up and down | - |
| Engine speed at cruise | 1,100-1,400 rpm on modern engines | - | - |
| Exhaust temperature during regen | 900-1,200 °F at the DPF | - | Over 1,300 °F |
| Fuel economy (loaded dry van, 65 mph) | 6.5-8.5 mpg | A drop of more than 0.5 mpg in a month | - |
| Idle share of engine hours | Under 20-25% | Over 35% | - |
## Electrical
| Reading | Normal | Means if wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Battery voltage, engine off | 12.6-12.8 V | 12.2 V is about half charged; under 12.0 V will not crank in the cold |
| Voltage while running | 13.8-14.4 V | Under 13.2 V: alternator not charging. Over 14.8 V: overcharging, boils batteries |
| Cranking voltage | Stays over 9.6 V | Drops lower: weak batteries or bad cables |
| Number of batteries | 3-4 group 31 batteries in parallel | One bad battery drags the others down |
## Air system
| Reading | Normal | Means if wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Governor cut-out (compressor stops) | 120-135 psi | Higher: governor fault, over-pressure |
| Governor cut-in (compressor starts) | No more than 25 psi below cut-out, around 100-110 psi | - |
| Build-up time from 85 to 100 psi | Under 45 seconds at 600-900 rpm | Slower: compressor or leak |
| Low air warning comes on | At or above 60 psi | Must come on by 60 psi |
| Spring brakes pop on | Between 20 and 45 psi | - |
| Leak-down, brakes released, engine off | Under 2 psi/min single, 3 psi/min combination | More: air leak, out of service if bad |
| Leak-down, brakes applied | Under 3 psi/min single, 4 psi/min combination | More: leak in service brake circuit |
## Brakes
| Chamber size | Readjustment limit (standard stroke) | Long-stroke limit |
|---|---|---|
| Type 20 | 1-3/4" | 2" |
| Type 24 | 1-3/4" | 2" (2-1/2" for long-stroke 3" rated) |
| Type 30 | 2" | 2-1/2" |
| Type 36 | 2-1/4" | - |
| Lining thickness (drum) | Out of service under 1/4" at the thinnest point on the shoe | - |
| Disc pad | Out of service under 1/8" | - |
## Tires
| Position | Usual pressure, cold | Legal tread minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Steer | 105-120 psi | 4/32" |
| Drive | 95-105 psi | 2/32" |
| Trailer | 95-105 psi | 2/32" |
| Wide-base single | 105-120 psi | 2/32" |
> note: Always use the load and inflation table for the actual tire and load. These are typical numbers for 11R22.5 and 295/75R22.5 tires on a loaded tractor-trailer.
## Fluids
| Fluid | Normal | Means if wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Engine oil level | Between the marks on a cold, level truck, checked 15 minutes after shutdown | Rising level: fuel or coolant getting into oil |
| Coolant | Between MIN and MAX on the tank, cold | Dropping with no leak: EGR cooler or head gasket |
| DEF | Over 10% | Under 5%: derate warnings start |
| DEF concentration | 32.5% urea (refractometer) | Diluted or wrong DEF: quality codes and derate |
| Coolant freeze point | -34 °F with 50/50 mix | Weaker: too much water |
| Fuel | Over 1/4 tank | Running under 1/8 risks air in the system |
## Samsara and Kaido readings
| Reading | Where | Normal |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel % | Samsara fuel level, Kaido fuel page | Rises 15% or more at a fill; drops 1% about every 2 gallons on a 200 gallon tank |
| DEF % | Samsara DEF level | Drops about 1 gallon per 40-50 gallons of diesel |
| Battery mV | Samsara battery voltage | 13,800-14,400 mV running |
| Coolant mC | Samsara coolant temperature in milli-degrees C | 85,000-95,000 (185-203 °F) |
| Oil pressure kPa | Samsara oil pressure | 100-450 kPa (15-65 psi) |
