# Cooling issues
Overheating, coolant loss, fans, belts and hoses.
::: issue Engine overheating | stop
dash: Temperature over 220 °F, red temperature lamp or stop lamp, derate.
looks: Steam from under the hood, coolant spraying, smell of hot coolant.
why: Low coolant (leak), fan not engaging, thermostat stuck shut, water pump failed, radiator clogged with dirt or bugs, belt broken.
do: Stop safely. Do not open the cap while hot: coolant at 15 psi and 230 °F burns badly. Let it cool 30-60 minutes. Road call if a leak or broken belt.
:::
::: issue Coolant leaking from a hose | soon
looks: Pink, orange or green wetness at a hose end or clamp, crust around the clamp, swollen or cracked hose, drips under the truck.
do: Replace hose and clamps ($100-400). Spring or constant-tension clamps on silicone hoses.
:::
::: issue Water pump leaking | soon
looks: Coolant crust or drip from the weep hole under the pump pulley. Wobbly pulley.
hear: Grinding or squeal from the pump bearing.
do: Replace pump ($800-2,000 installed).
:::
::: issue Radiator leaking | soon
looks: Coolant stain or crust at the plastic tanks where they meet the aluminum core, or a wet spot in the fins.
do: Replace radiator ($1,500-3,500 installed). Leak stop products are a short-term patch.
:::
::: issue Radiator or charge air cooler fins clogged | watch
looks: Bugs, dirt, cottonwood fluff or plastic bags packed in the front of the grille. Fins bent flat.
dash: Runs hot on hills and in summer.
do: Clean with low-pressure water from the back side. Do not bend the fins.
:::
::: issue Fan clutch not engaging | soon
looks: Fan does not come on loud (roar) when the engine is hot, or fan always on (roar and fuel use).
why: Air-operated fan clutch: air leak, solenoid or worn friction lining. Viscous clutch: failed.
do: Shop. $500-1,500.
:::
::: issue Thermostat stuck | soon
dash: Stuck shut: overheats. Stuck open: engine never warms up past 160-170 °F, no heat in the cab, poor fuel economy, regen problems.
do: Replace ($200-600 installed, usually two thermostats).
:::
::: issue Coolant loss with no visible leak | soon
looks: Coolant tank keeps dropping, nothing under the truck. Sometimes white smoke or coolant smell from the exhaust, coolant tank gets pressurized or bubbles.
why: EGR cooler leaking into the intake, head gasket, oil cooler (coolant in oil), heater core (wet cab floor, fog on windows).
do: Pressure test and shop. Do not keep topping off for weeks.
:::
::: issue Coolant contaminated or wrong type | watch
looks: Coolant rusty brown, sludgy, oily, or mixed colors (green with orange).
why: Mixing coolant types, no maintenance, oil cooler leak.
do: Test strips for freeze point and additive level. Flush if contaminated ($300-600).
:::
::: issue Belt worn, cracked or squealing | soon
looks: Cracks across the ribs, missing chunks, shiny glazed sides, threads hanging, belt off center on a pulley.
hear: Squeal on start-up or when the AC compressor or fan kicks in.
do: Replace belt ($100-300). Check tensioner and idler pulleys at the same time. A thrown belt stops the water pump on many engines.
:::
::: issue Belt tensioner or idler bearing failing | soon
looks: Tensioner arm shaking or bouncing; pulley wobbles; black dust under the pulley.
hear: Chirp, rattle or grinding.
do: Replace ($150-400 installed).
:::
::: issue Coolant overflow tank cracked | soon
looks: Crack or wet seam on the plastic surge tank; cap seal torn.
do: Replace tank ($200-500 installed) or cap ($30).
:::
