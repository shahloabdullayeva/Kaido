# ELD, telematics and camera issues
Samsara gateways, ELD malfunctions, dash cameras and what Kaido shows when they fail.
::: issue Truck not reporting in Samsara | soon
looks: Samsara and Kaido show the truck's last location hours or days old, fuel % and engine data stuck. Kaido raises "ELD not reporting".
why: Gateway unplugged or loose at the 9-pin diagnostic port, power cable damaged, truck parked with the batteries disconnected, cellular dead zone, gateway failed.
ask: Is the truck running? Is the light on the gateway (under the dash) lit? Was anything plugged in at the diagnostic port?
do: Re-seat the cable. If still offline, Samsara support swaps the gateway. Positions, fuel and safety alerts in Kaido stop until it reports again.
:::
::: issue ELD malfunction | soon
looks: Driver app shows a malfunction (letter code like P, E, S, T, L, R, O), or the driver says the logs are not recording.
why: Lost engine data (power, engine sync), timing, storage, transfer.
do: Driver must note the malfunction and switch to paper logs if not fixed within 8 days per FMCSA rules; the carrier has 8 days to fix. Call Samsara support.
:::
::: issue Dash camera offline or blocked | watch
looks: Camera not recording, lens covered, camera pointing at the ceiling, "obstructed camera" events.
why: Driver covered it, cable loose, power.
do: Check mounting and cable. Kaido shows obstructed camera events in the safety alerts.
:::
::: issue Fuel % reading wrong | watch
looks: Kaido fuel % does not move after a fill, or jumps around while parked.
why: Truck's fuel sender, unbalanced tanks, gateway not reading the fuel level from the engine computer.
do: Compare with the dash gauge. If the dash is right and Samsara wrong, call Samsara support.
:::
::: issue Odometer jumps or wrong | watch
looks: Odometer in Kaido differs from the dash, or jumps.
why: Samsara switched between GPS and engine odometer, gateway swapped.
do: Set an odometer reading on the truck page in Kaido from a photo of the dash.
:::
