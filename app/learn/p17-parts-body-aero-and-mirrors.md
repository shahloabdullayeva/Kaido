# Body, aero and mirrors
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hood | Tilts forward over the engine; fiberglass | Cracks, collision damage | $3,000 to $8,000 |
| Hood latches and hood cylinder | Hold and lift the hood | Won't latch | $100 to $400 |
| Grille | Front of the hood | Broken | $300 to $1,000 |
| Front bumper | Front | Collision damage | $800 to $2,500 |
| Bumper air dam | Under the bumper, aero | Broken, scraped | $200 to $600 |
| Roof fairing | On top of the cab, aero | Cracked, loose | $800 to $2,500 |
| Side fairings (cab extenders) | Behind the doors, closes gap to trailer | Broken, missing | $300 to $1,000 each |
| Chassis skirts | Cover the fuel tanks and frame | Cracked, hanging | $300 to $1,000 each |
| Fenders and fender brackets | Over the front wheels and drive tires | Cracked, broken brackets | $200 to $800 |
| Mirrors (main, heated, motorized) | Doors | Broken glass, dead motor | Glass $30 to $100; assembly $150 to $600 |
| Hood mirrors / convex mirrors | On hood or fenders | Broken | $50 to $200 |
| Steps and grab handles | Getting into the cab | Loose, broken | $100 to $400 |
| Battery box and cover | Under the step | Cover missing, broken | $50 to $300 |
| Mud flaps | Behind drive tires | Missing, torn (violation in many states) | $20 to $60 each part |
| Mud flap hangers / brackets | Hold the flaps | Bent, broken | $30 to $100 part |
| Sun visor (exterior) | Above the windshield | Loose, rattling | $200 to $800 |
| Paint and decals, DOT number | Doors must show USDOT number | Missing number (violation) | Decals $50 to $200 |
