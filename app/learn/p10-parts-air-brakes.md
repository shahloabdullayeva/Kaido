# Air brakes
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Air compressor | On the engine, makes air | Slow air build, oil in air system | $1,500 to $3,500 |
| Governor | Starts and stops the compressor | Wrong cut in / cut out | $100 to $300 |
| Air dryer | Frame rail near the cab | Water in tanks, constant purging | Cartridge $50 to $150; dryer $600 to $1,500 |
| Air tanks (reservoirs) | Under the frame | Rust, leaks | $300 to $800 each |
| Tank drain valves (manual or automatic) | Bottom of each tank | Leaking or stuck drain | $50 to $250 |
| Safety (pop off) valve | Stops over pressure | Blowing off air | $50 to $150 |
| Air pressure gauges | Dash, primary and secondary | Wrong readings | $100 to $300 |
| Low air warning switch | Triggers buzzer and light | No warning | $50 to $150 |
| Foot valve (brake pedal valve) | Under the brake pedal | Air leak at pedal, weak brakes | $300 to $800 |
| Parking brake valve (yellow knob) | Dash | Won't hold in or release | $150 to $400 |
| Tractor protection valve / trailer supply valve (red knob) | Dash and behind the cab | Trailer brakes won't release | $200 to $500 |
| Relay valves | Near the axles; speed up brake application | Slow or dragging brakes | $150 to $400 |
| Quick release valves | Dump air fast when brakes release | Dragging brakes | $50 to $200 |
| Brake chambers (service) | Steer axle | Air leak, cracked diaphragm | $100 to $300 each |
| Spring brake chambers | Drive and trailer axles; also the parking brake | Leak, broken spring (dangerous) | $150 to $350 each |
| Pushrods | Chamber to slack adjuster | Bent, excess stroke | Done with chamber |
| Slack adjusters (automatic) | Arm connecting pushrod to S cam | Out of adjustment (top violation) | $100 to $200 each |
| S cam and bushings | Spreads the brake shoes | Worn bushings, sticking | $300 to $800 per wheel |
| Brake shoes / linings | Friction material inside the drum | Thin lining, oil soaked | Reline $500 to $1,000 per axle |
| Brake drums | Steel drum the shoes press against | Cracked, heat checked, out of round | $150 to $400 each |
| Return springs and hardware | Pull shoes back | Broken spring, dragging brake | $50 to $150 per wheel kit |
| Air disc brake pads and rotors | Newer trucks | Pad wear, rotor cracks | $800 to $1,600 per axle |
| Brake air lines and hoses | Carry air everywhere | Chafed, leaking (roadside violation) | $50 to $300 each |
| Gladhands and seals | Tractor to trailer air | Leaking seal | Seal $2 to $10; gladhand $30 to $80 |
| Air lines to trailer (red and blue) | Coiled hoses behind the cab | Cracked, rubbed | $80 to $200 set |
| ABS wheel speed sensors | At each wheel | ABS light | $100 to $300 each |
| ABS modulator valves | Near the wheels | ABS light, uneven braking | $200 to $500 each |
