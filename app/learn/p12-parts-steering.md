# Steering
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steering wheel and column | In the cab | Loose, excess play | Column $500 to $1,500 |
| Steering shaft and U joints | Column to the steering gear | Clunk, binding | $300 to $800 |
| Steering gear (steering box) | On the frame near the front axle | Leaks, loose steering | $1,200 to $3,000 |
| Power steering pump | On the engine (often gear driven) | Whine, hard steering | $600 to $1,500 |
| Power steering reservoir and filter | Near the engine | Low fluid, foamy | $100 to $400 |
| Power steering hoses | Pump to gear | Leaks | $150 to $500 |
| Pitman arm | Steering gear to drag link | Loose, bent | $200 to $500 |
| Drag link | Pitman arm to the steering knuckle | Worn ends, play | $300 to $700 |
| Tie rod and tie rod ends | Connect both front wheels | Play, uneven tire wear | $150 to $400 per end |
| Castle nuts and cotter pins | Lock the linkage joints | Missing pin (violation) | $5 to $20 |
| Steering knuckles / steering arms | Front wheel pivots | Damage, play | $800 to $2,000 |
| Kingpins and bushings | Pivot the front wheels turn on | Shaky steering, tire wear | $800 to $1,500 per axle |
| Front end alignment | Wheel angles | Tire wear, pulling | $200 to $350 |
