# HVAC and APU
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| AC compressor and clutch | On the engine, belt driven | No cold air, clutch won't engage | $400 to $1,200 |
| Condenser | In front of the radiator | Debris damage, leaks | $500 to $1,200 |
| Receiver drier / accumulator | Removes moisture | Replaced when system opened | $80 to $250 |
| Expansion valve / orifice tube | Meters refrigerant | Frosting, no cooling | $200 to $600 |
| Evaporator | Inside the HVAC box | Leaks, smell | $800 to $2,000 (dash work) |
| AC lines and hoses | Refrigerant plumbing | Leaks, the most common AC problem | $150 to $600 |
| AC pressure switches | Protect the system | Compressor won't run | $50 to $200 |
| Refrigerant recharge (R-134a / R-1234yf) | Refill after a leak | Warm air | $150 to $350 |
| Blower motor and resistor | Pushes air into the cab | No air, one speed only | $200 to $500 |
| Blend door actuators | Hot and cold mix | Stuck temperature | $100 to $400 |
| HVAC control panel | Dash controls | Unresponsive | $200 to $600 |
| Cabin air filter | Filters air into the cab | Weak airflow, smell | $20 to $60 |
| Sleeper HVAC unit | Separate AC and heat for the bunk | Not cooling | $300 to $1,500 |
| Bunk heater (Espar / Webasto) | Diesel fired heater | Won't light, smoke | Service $150 to $400; unit $1,200 to $2,500 |
| Diesel APU (TriPac, ComfortPro) | Small engine on the frame for AC, heat, power | Won't start, not cooling | Service $150 to $300; major $500 to $2,000 |
| Electric / battery APU | Battery powered AC | Short run time, battery faults | $500 to $3,000 repairs |
