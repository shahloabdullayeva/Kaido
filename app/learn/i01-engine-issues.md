# Engine issues
Hard starts, knocks, smoke, power loss and leaks, with what each looks and sounds like.
## Starting
::: issue Cranks but will not start | soon
looks: Engine turns over at normal speed but never fires. Maybe a little white smoke from the stack, or none.
ask: How much fuel? Did it run out? Is it cold (under 20 °F)? Was the fuel filter just changed? Any codes?
why: Out of fuel or air in the fuel system after running dry, gelled fuel in cold weather, clogged fuel filter, failed lift pump, low rail pressure (high-pressure pump, regulator, leaking injector), crank or cam sensor, immobilizer.
check: Fuel level, prime the system with the hand primer or electric primer, look at fuel filter bowl for wax or water, read codes (SPN 157 rail pressure, SPN 636/723 sensors).
do: Prime and retry. In cold weather use anti-gel and a heated garage or a road-service fuel warmer. Road call if not.
cost: Road call $250-500; fuel filter $80-200 parts; crank sensor $150-400 installed.
:::
::: issue Will not crank, only clicks | soon
looks: Turn the key, dash lights dim or go out, a click or rapid clicking, starter does not turn.
why: Batteries flat (lights left on, APU drain, cold), corroded or loose cable, bad starter solenoid or starter, failed ground strap.
check: Battery voltage (12.6 V full; under 12.0 V too low), cable ends for white or green corrosion, warm cable after cranking.
do: Jump start or road service. Then test batteries and charging at a shop.
cost: Jump $150-300; group 31 battery $180-300 each; starter $500-1,200 installed.
:::
::: issue Starts hard, long crank | watch
looks: Has to crank 5-15 seconds before it catches; white smoke on start-up.
why: Air leaking into fuel lines (drains back overnight), weak batteries cranking slowly, intake heater not working in cold, low compression, leaking injector.
check: Does it start fine after sitting a short time but not overnight? That points to fuel drain-back (check valve, loose filter).
do: Shop visit at next stop.
:::
## Power and running
::: issue Low power, no codes | soon
feel: Truck pulls weak on hills, slow to pick up speed, no limit on top speed.
looks: Sometimes black smoke under load.
why: Clogged air filter (restriction indicator in the red), boost leak, plugged fuel filter, turbo wastegate or VGT actuator stuck, exhaust restriction (DPF plugged), dragging brake.
check: Air filter restriction gauge, charge air cooler hoses and clamps (oily film around a leak), fuel filter age.
do: Shop. Air filter is the cheap first check.
cost: Air filter $60-150; CAC boot and clamps $100-300; charge air cooler $900-2,000 installed.
:::
::: issue Derate: speed limited to 55 or 5 mph | soon
feel: Truck will not go over a set speed, power cut, often after several warnings.
dash: Check engine plus DEF, DPF or MIL lamps. Message such as "Engine derate", "Speed limited", "Emissions system fault".
why: Almost always aftertreatment: DEF quality or level, DEF doser or pump, NOx sensor, DPF soot load too high, failed regen. See [Aftertreatment issues](/learn/aftertreatment-issues).
do: Read the code. Some derates reset after a key cycle or a successful regen. 5 mph derate needs a tow or mobile repair.
:::
::: issue Surging or hunting at idle | watch
feel: Engine speed goes up and down by itself at idle or at steady cruise.
why: Air in the fuel, clogged fuel filter, fuel pressure regulator, failing sensor (throttle pedal, rail pressure), low fuel.
do: Change fuel filters, check for air in clear lines. Shop if it stays.
:::
::: issue Engine shuts down by itself | stop
looks: Stop engine lamp, 30 second countdown, engine dies. May not restart, or restarts and shuts down again.
why: Engine protection: low oil pressure, high coolant temperature, low coolant level, high intake temperature. Also running out of fuel, idle shutdown timer (normal, after 5-15 minutes idling).
check: What did the screen say? Check oil and coolant levels cold.
do: Do not keep restarting a protection shutdown. Road call.
:::
::: issue Knocking from the engine | stop
hear: Deep, heavy knock that gets faster with engine speed, louder under load. Different from the light diesel clatter.
why: Rod or main bearing failure, piston or wrist pin, injector stuck open (fuel knock), broken valve spring.
check: Oil level and oil pressure reading. Metal in the oil filter.
do: Stop and shut down. Tow. Driving on a bearing knock ends in a thrown rod.
cost: In-frame overhaul $18,000-35,000; replacement engine $35,000-60,000.
:::
::: issue Engine overheats on hills | soon
dash: Temperature gauge climbs above 215 °F on grades, then comes back down.
why: Fan clutch not engaging, clogged radiator or charge air cooler fins (bugs, dirt), low coolant, thermostat sticking, water pump wearing.
do: Shop soon, before summer or a long grade. See [Cooling issues](/learn/cooling-issues).
:::
## Smoke
::: issue Black smoke | soon
looks: Black puffs or a black trail from the stack when accelerating. On newer trucks, soot inside the stack tip.
why: Boost leak, dirty air filter, EGR valve stuck, leaking injector, turbo problem. On a truck with a DPF, black smoke out the stack means the DPF itself is cracked or removed.
do: Shop soon. A cracked DPF is a big bill and an emissions violation.
:::
::: issue White smoke, sweet smell | stop
looks: Thick white smoke that hangs in the air, keeps coming after warm-up. Coolant level drops.
smell: Sweet, like syrup.
why: Coolant entering the cylinders: EGR cooler leak (most common on 2007+ engines), head gasket, cracked head.
do: Stop. Do not drive if coolant is dropping. Coolant in the oil wipes out bearings.
cost: EGR cooler $2,500-5,500 installed; head gasket $5,000-10,000.
:::
::: issue Blue smoke | soon
looks: Blue-grey haze from the stack, oil around the turbo outlet, oil dripping from the stack on long idles (called slobber or wet stacking).
why: Turbo seals leaking, worn rings or liners, crankcase breather plugged, overfilled oil, long idling.
check: Oil use per 1,000 miles. A healthy engine uses under 1 quart per 2,000-5,000 miles.
do: Shop. Watch the oil level every fuel stop until then.
:::
## Leaks and fluids
::: issue Oil in the coolant | stop
looks: Brown, oily film or chocolate-milk color in the coolant tank.
why: Failed oil cooler (most common), head gasket.
do: Stop. Flush and repair. Cost $1,500-4,000 for an oil cooler with flush.
:::
::: issue Coolant in the oil | stop
looks: Oil on the dipstick is milky, tan or grey, like a milkshake. Oil level may rise.
why: EGR cooler, oil cooler, head gasket, liner seals, cracked head.
do: Do not run it. Oil sample, pressure test, repair.
:::
::: issue Fuel in the oil | soon
looks: Oil level rising between changes, oil thin and smells of diesel.
why: Leaking injector or injector cup, fuel pump seal, many failed regens (post-injection fuel washes into oil).
do: Oil sample, injector test. Thin oil damages bearings.
:::
::: issue Crankcase pressure (blow-by) | soon
looks: Smoke or vapor puffing from the breather tube or dipstick tube, oil pushed out of the dipstick tube.
why: Worn rings or liners, turbo leaking into the crankcase, clogged breather filter.
do: Shop blow-by test. Change breather filter first ($50-150).
:::
