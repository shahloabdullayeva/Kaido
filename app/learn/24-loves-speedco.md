# Love's Truck Care and Speedco services
Love's runs two service brands at its truck stops: **Love's Truck Care** (tires, light mechanical, roadside) and **Speedco** (fast oil and PM lanes).

## Overview
Love's runs two service brands at its truck stops: **Love's Truck Care** (tires, light mechanical, roadside) and **Speedco** (fast oil and PM lanes). Both do the same kinds of work, and their invoices come through the same way. These are the service groups they list:

| Service | What it includes | What you'll see on the invoice |
|---|---|---|
| Oil and PM | Oil filled to the engine maker's capacity, oil filter, fuel filters, fluid level checks, chassis lube. Oils stocked: 15W-40, 10W-30, 5W-40 (CK-4) | Oil by the gallon, each filter as a line, grease or lube, labor or a package price, environmental or disposal fee |
| Transmission, differential and gearbox service | Drain all fluid, refill to capacity, visual check | Gear oil or transmission fluid by the gallon, labor |
| Reefer and APU PM | PM on the reefer unit or APU engine | Oil, filters, labor; reefer PM is by engine hours |
| Commercial truck tires | New tires (Bridgestone, Continental, Goodyear, Yokohama and others), Love's retreads, mounting, repairs | Tire (brand, size, position), mount and dismount, valve stem, balance, disposal fee, casing credit if a casing is traded in |
| Light mechanical | AC, brakes, computer diagnostics, DOT inspections, driveline, electrical, exhaust, suspension, tire repair | Parts lines plus labor hours; diagnostics as its own line |
| Batteries | Free battery testing; Interstate commercial batteries | Battery, core charge (refunded when the old battery is returned), labor |
| Roadside assistance | 1,200+ service trucks, 24/7, anywhere in the US | Service call fee, mileage, labor, parts or tire |
| Warranty and recalls | Freightliner ExpressPoint and International dealer backed warranty and recall work at select locations | Often $0 to the carrier if covered; check before paying |
| Truck wash | Automated wash bays at some locations | Wash |

**Things to watch on these invoices:** oil gallons should roughly match the engine's capacity (10 to 15 gal); a "PM" price should include the filters it claims; tire lines should state size, brand, position and new vs retread; battery core charges should be refunded when the old battery goes back; and warranty eligible work on newer Freightliners and Internationals should go through ExpressPoint or warranty rather than being paid.
