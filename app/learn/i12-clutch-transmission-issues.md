# Clutch and transmission issues
Manual, automated manual (I-Shift, DT12, Endurant, UltraShift) and Allison automatic problems.
## Clutch
::: issue Clutch slipping | soon
feel: Engine revs up but the truck does not speed up the same, worst on hills and loaded.
smell: Burnt clutch smell (like burnt paper).
why: Worn clutch, out of adjustment, oil on the disc (rear main seal or transmission input seal leak).
do: Adjust if adjustable; otherwise clutch replacement ($2,500-5,000 installed).
:::
::: issue Clutch will not release | soon
feel: Hard to get into gear when stopped, truck creeps with the pedal down, grinding.
why: Linkage, release bearing, clutch brake worn, hydraulic leak.
do: Shop.
:::
::: issue Clutch needs calibration (automated) | soon
dash: Transmission lamp, message to calibrate clutch, slow or harsh engagement.
why: Clutch wear in automated transmissions needs the computer to learn the new point.
do: Calibration with a laptop ($150-300). Sometimes the driver can do it from the dash.
:::
## Automated and manual transmissions
::: issue Will not shift or stuck in gear (automated) | soon
dash: Transmission lamp, "N" flashing, gear display shows dashes.
why: Low air pressure (shifters use air), shift actuator, clutch actuator, sensor, low battery voltage, data link.
check: Air pressure over 100 psi, batteries good. Key off for 2 minutes and restart.
do: Code read. Many trucks have a limp mode that gives a few gears to get off the road.
:::
::: issue Grinding when shifting (manual) | soon
hear: Grinding as the gear goes in.
why: Not double clutching, clutch brake worn, clutch not releasing.
do: Driver technique first; then shop.
:::
::: issue Jumps out of gear | soon
feel: Shifter pops back to neutral under load or coasting.
why: Worn gears or shift forks, loose mounts, worn detents.
do: Shop. Dangerous on hills.
:::
::: issue Transmission oil leak | soon
looks: Oil dripping from the transmission: front seal (between engine and transmission), rear seal at the driveshaft yoke, cooler lines, case.
do: Check level. Front seal oil can soak the clutch.
:::
::: issue Transmission overheating | soon
dash: Transmission temperature warning, Allison "check trans".
smell: Hot oil.
why: Low oil, cooler clogged, slipping, heavy loads at low speed.
do: Stop and cool. Shop.
:::
::: issue Whine or howl from the transmission | watch
hear: Whine that changes with gear and road speed.
why: Worn bearing or gears, low oil.
do: Check oil level and magnetic drain plug for metal.
:::
