# Lubrication
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engine oil (10 to 15 gal) | Lubricates, cools, carries soot | Low level, milky, fuel smell | In the oil change, $400 to $600 |
| Oil filter(s) | Full flow, sometimes plus bypass | Changed every PM | $30 to $80 each part |
| Oil pump | Pushes oil through the engine | Low oil pressure light (stop the engine) | $1,500 to $4,000 |
| Oil cooler | Cools oil with coolant | Oil in coolant or coolant in oil | $1,500 to $3,500 |
| Oil pressure sensor | Feeds the gauge and ECM | False low oil pressure warnings | $100 to $300 |
| Dipstick and tube | Checking oil level | Broken tube, leaks | $50 to $200 |
| Oil drain plug and gasket | Bottom of the pan | Drips after oil change | $20 to $80 |
| Grease fittings (zerks) | On kingpins, U joints, slack adjusters, spring pins, fifth wheel | Broken fitting, parts not taking grease | $10 to $30 each |
| Automatic lube system | Some trucks grease themselves | Empty reservoir, broken lines | $300 to $1,500 repairs |
