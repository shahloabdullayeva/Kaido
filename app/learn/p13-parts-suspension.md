# Suspension
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Front leaf springs | Steer axle | Broken or shifted leaf (violation) | $300 to $800 each |
| Rear leaf springs | Older or vocational trucks | Broken leaf | $300 to $900 each |
| Spring hangers and shackles | Hold springs to the frame | Cracks, worn | $150 to $500 |
| Spring pins and bushings | Pivot points | Clunk, play | $150 to $300 per set |
| U bolts | Clamp springs to the axle | Loose, missing | $50 to $150 each |
| Air bags (air springs) | Drive axles and trailer | Leaks, sagging, torn | $200 to $400 each |
| Leveling valve (height control valve) | Keeps ride height | Truck too high or too low, leaking air | $150 to $400 |
| Shock absorbers | Every axle | Leaking oil, bouncing ride | $150 to $300 each |
| Torque rods | Hold drive axles in place | Worn bushings, clunk | $200 to $500 each |
| Trailing arms / beams | Air ride frame on drive axles | Cracks, bushing wear | $500 to $1,500 |
| Walking beams | Some vocational trucks | Center bushing wear | $800 to $2,000 |
| Cab air bags / cab shocks | Under the cab | Bouncy cab ride | $150 to $400 each |
| Seat air suspension | Driver's seat | Seat sinks, leaks air | $200 to $600 |
