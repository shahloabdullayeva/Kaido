# Driveline and axles
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main driveshaft | Transmission to the front drive axle | Vibration, clunk | $800 to $2,000 |
| Interaxle driveshaft | Between the two drive axles | Vibration | $500 to $1,500 |
| U joints | Flexible joints on driveshafts | Clunk, rust dust, vibration | $200 to $500 each |
| Carrier (center) bearing | Supports a two piece driveshaft | Vibration, noise | $200 to $500 |
| Yokes | Driveshaft attachment points | Wear, play | $150 to $400 |
| Front drive axle differential (with power divider) | Front of the two drive axles | Whine, leaks, overheating | Service $250 to $500; rebuild $1,000 to $5,000 |
| Rear drive axle differential | Rear drive axle | Whine, leaks | Same as above |
| Interaxle differential (power divider) and lock | Splits power between drive axles | Grinding, lock stuck | $1,500 to $4,000 |
| Differential lock (diff lock) | Locks wheels for slippery roads | Won't engage | $300 to $1,000 |
| Axle shafts | Diff to the wheel hubs | Broken shaft (truck won't move) | $400 to $1,200 |
| Axle shaft gasket | Seals axle shaft to hub | Gear oil leak on the wheel | $100 to $300 |
| Axle housing and breather | Holds the diff and shafts | Leaks, blocked breather causing seal leaks | Breather $20 to $80 |
| Pinion seal | Where the driveshaft enters the diff | Oil leak | $300 to $800 |
| Gear oil service | Drain and refill diffs | Done every few hundred thousand miles | $250 to $500 |
