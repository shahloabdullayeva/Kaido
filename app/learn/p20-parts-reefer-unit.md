# Reefer unit
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
A reefer is a refrigerated trailer. The cooling unit on its front (Thermo King or Carrier) has its own small diesel engine, fuel tank, battery and PM schedule, usually counted by **engine hours**, not miles. A failed reefer on a loaded trailer is an emergency because the freight can spoil.

| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reefer unit (whole) | Front of the trailer | Won't hold temperature | New $20,000 to $35,000 |
| Reefer diesel engine | Inside the unit, runs the compressor | Won't start, smoke, overheating | Rebuild $4,000 to $10,000 |
| Reefer PM service | Oil, filters, belts, check refrigerant | Due by engine hours | $300 to $700 |
| Reefer fuel tank | Under the trailer | Leaks, empty | $500 to $1,500 |
| Reefer battery | Inside the unit | Won't start | $150 to $300 |
| Refrigeration compressor | Inside the unit | Not cooling, noise | $2,500 to $6,000 |
| Condenser coil and fan | Front of the unit | Dirty coil, weak cooling | $500 to $2,000 |
| Evaporator coil and fans | Inside the trailer at the front | Iced up, not cooling | $1,000 to $3,000 |
| Belts | Drive the fans and alternator | Squeal, thrown belt | $100 to $300 |
| Controller / display (microprocessor) | Sets and logs temperature | Alarms, blank screen | $1,500 to $4,000 |
| Temperature sensors | Measure return and discharge air | Wrong readings, alarms | $100 to $300 |
| Defrost system / heater | Melts ice off the evaporator | Iced coil | $300 to $1,000 |
| Refrigerant leak repair and recharge | Lines and fittings | Low refrigerant alarm | $300 to $1,500 |
| Alternator and starter (reefer) | Unit electrical | Charging or start faults | $300 to $800 |
| Air chute | Fabric duct along the ceiling | Torn, poor airflow | $300 to $800 |
| Bulkhead | Wall at the front of the trailer for airflow | Damaged | $300 to $1,000 |
