# Sounds, smells and feelings
What a driver hears, smells or feels, and what it usually points to.
## How to use this
Drivers describe problems in their own words. Ask: **when** does it happen (cold start, accelerating, braking, turning, idling, only loaded), **where** does it come from (front, under the cab, a wheel, the trailer), and **does it change with engine speed or road speed**. Engine-speed noises come from the engine and its belts. Road-speed noises come from tires, wheel ends, driveline and axles.
## Sounds
| What the driver hears | When | Usually means | Urgency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loud hiss that does not stop | Parked or driving | Air leak: line, fitting, brake chamber, gladhand seal, air bag | Big leak: stop. Small: today |
| Short "pssht" every few minutes | Parked with engine running | Air dryer purging. Normal | None |
| Air dryer purging every few seconds | Running | Air leak somewhere, governor or dryer fault | Today |
| Squeal at start-up, goes away | Cold start | Belt slipping (glazed or loose), tensioner | Watch |
| Squeal that stays | Running | Belt worn, idler pulley or tensioner bearing failing | Soon. A thrown belt stops the water pump and alternator |
| Chirp or squeak | Idle | Idler pulley or tensioner bearing | Soon |
| Whistle that rises with boost | Accelerating | Boost leak: charge air cooler, intake clamp, cracked boot | Soon |
| Siren-like whine or howl from the turbo | Accelerating | Turbo bearing failing | Soon. A turbo that lets go can send pieces into the engine |
| Puffing or chuffing at the manifold | Cold start, under load | Exhaust manifold gasket or cracked manifold | Watch |
| Deep knock that rises with engine speed | Running | Rod bearing, main bearing, injector, piston | Stop. Do not drive |
| Light ticking | Idle | Valve lash, injector noise (some is normal on diesels), exhaust leak | Watch |
| Grinding when shifting | Shifting (manual) | Clutch brake, clutch not releasing, synchronizer-style wear | Soon |
| Clunk when taking off or stopping | Low speed | U-joint, slack in driveline, torque rods, fifth wheel slack, spring hangers | Soon |
| Whine that changes with road speed | Highway | Differential gears or pinion bearing, often low gear oil | Soon |
| Growl or hum from one wheel, louder in a turn | Driving | Wheel bearing | Today. A failed bearing can let the wheel come off |
| Grinding or scraping when braking | Braking | Brake lining worn to metal | Today. Out of service |
| Squeal when braking | Braking | Glazed or contaminated lining, worn drum | Watch |
| Thumping that speeds up with road speed | Driving | Flat spot, tread separation, out-of-round tire, cupping | Separation: stop |
| Rattle under the cab | Bumps | Loose heat shield, exhaust clamp, cab mount | Watch |
| Popping or banging from the trailer | Turning tight | Trailer tandem slider not locked, worn suspension, normal tire scrub | Check slider pins today |
| Bang then flapping | Driving | Tire blowout | Stop safely |
| Clicking, will not crank | Turning key | Batteries flat, bad cable or starter solenoid | Road call |
## Smells
| What the driver smells | Usually means | Urgency |
|---|---|---|
| Sweet syrup smell, fog on the windshield inside | Heater core leaking coolant | Soon |
| Sweet smell outside, steam | Coolant leaking on hot engine | Today, stop if level drops |
| Hot oil or burning oil | Oil leaking on exhaust or turbo | Today. Fire risk |
| Raw diesel | Fuel leak, loose filter, injector line, crossover line | Large: stop |
| Burning rubber or hot brakes | Dragging brake, stuck spring brake, slipping belt, duals rubbing | Stop and check which wheel is hot |
| Burning clutch (like burnt paper) | Clutch slipping | Soon |
| Electrical burning, plastic | Wiring short, alternator, blower motor | Stop and shut off. Fire risk |
| Rotten eggs | Gear oil leaking, or battery overcharging (sulfur) | Battery: today |
| Ammonia | DEF leak or over-dosing | Soon |
| Exhaust fumes in the cab | Exhaust leak under the cab, door seals, sleeper leak | Today. Carbon monoxide risk |
## Feelings
| What the driver feels | Usually means | Urgency |
|---|---|---|
| Loss of power, will not go over 55 mph | Aftertreatment derate (stage 1) | Today |
| Will not go over 5 mph | Aftertreatment derate (final stage) | Road call or tow |
| Surging or bucking | Fuel filter, air in fuel, gelled fuel in cold, sensor | Soon |
| Shake in the steering at 50-65 mph | Steer tire balance, bent wheel, cupped tire | Soon |
| Vibration through the seat that rises with speed | Driveline: U-joint, carrier bearing, out-of-phase shaft | Soon |
| Vibration only when accelerating | Driveline angle, U-joint | Soon |
| Pull to one side while driving | Alignment, low tire, brake dragging | Soon |
| Pull to one side when braking | Brake out of adjustment, contaminated lining, chamber | Today |
| Wandering, steering loose, lots of play | Kingpins, tie rod ends, steering box, drag link | Today |
| Steering heavy | Power steering pump, low fluid, steering gear | Today |
| Pedal goes far down, weak brakes | Brakes out of adjustment, slack adjuster | Today. Out of service |
| Truck leans or rides low on one side | Air bag leak, height control valve, broken spring | Soon |
| Hard bouncing | Worn shocks, broken leaf spring | Soon |
| Clutch pedal goes to the floor, will not release | Clutch linkage, release bearing | Road call |
| Trailer pushes or pulls on the tractor | Trailer brake problem, gladhand seals, relay valve | Today |
