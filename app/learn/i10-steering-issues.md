# Steering issues
Play, pull, wander, heavy steering and leaks.
::: issue Steering wheel play too much | soon
feel: Wheel turns 2 inches or more (on a 20" wheel) before the tires move. Truck wanders, driver keeps correcting.
why: Worn tie rod ends, drag link ends, steering gear, loose gear mount, worn kingpins.
check: With the engine running, one person turns the wheel side to side while another watches each joint for movement.
dot: Free play over the limit (about 2-1/4" on a 20" wheel for power steering) is out of service.
do: Shop. Replace worn parts and align.
:::
::: issue Kingpins worn | soon
looks: Front tire top moves in and out when you rock it with the axle lifted. Uneven steer tire wear.
feel: Wander, shimmy.
do: Kingpin kit $800-1,800 per axle installed.
:::
::: issue Tie rod end or drag link worn | soon
looks: Torn rubber boot, grease leaking, joint moves when the wheel is turned.
do: Replace ($150-500 per part installed) and align.
:::
::: issue Power steering fluid leak | soon
looks: Red or amber oily fluid at the steering gear, pump or hoses; wet hoses; low reservoir.
feel: Steering heavy, whining when turning.
do: Find and fix. Low fluid ruins the pump.
:::
::: issue Power steering pump failing | soon
hear: Whine or groan that gets louder when turning.
feel: Steering heavy, worse at idle.
do: Replace pump ($600-1,500 installed).
:::
::: issue Steering gear leaking or loose | soon
looks: Oil wet around the gear box on the frame rail, cracked frame near the gear mount, loose mounting bolts.
dot: Loose steering gear mount is out of service.
do: Shop. Gear $1,500-3,500 installed.
:::
::: issue Pulls to one side | soon
feel: Truck drifts left or right on a flat road, driver holds the wheel turned.
why: Low tire, alignment, dragging brake, worn parts, mismatched steer tires, road crown.
do: Check pressure, then alignment.
:::
::: issue Shimmy or death wobble | soon
feel: Steering wheel shakes violently after a bump at certain speeds.
why: Out-of-balance steer tire, worn kingpins or tie rods, bad shock.
do: Balance and inspect.
:::
::: issue Steering wheel off center | watch
looks: Wheel tilted when driving straight.
why: Alignment done without centering the wheel, bent part.
do: Alignment.
:::
