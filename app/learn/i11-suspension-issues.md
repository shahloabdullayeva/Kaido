# Suspension issues
Air bags, height control valves, springs, shocks, torque rods and U-bolts.
::: issue Air bag leaking or flat | soon
looks: One side of the truck or trailer sits low, bag folded or not round, cracks in the rubber, bag bulging oddly.
hear: Hiss at the bag.
do: Replace bag ($150-400 installed). A flat bag means harsh ride and damage to the frame and cargo.
dot: Air bag deflated or leaking is a violation; missing or broken bag can be out of service.
:::
::: issue Height control valve fault | soon
looks: Truck sits too high or too low, leans, or the air bags dump and fill constantly (air dryer pops often).
why: Linkage arm broken or bent, valve leaking.
do: Replace valve or arm ($150-400). Check ride height to spec.
:::
::: issue Broken leaf spring | soon
looks: A leaf cracked or shifted out of line, pieces missing, gap between leaves.
feel: Truck leans, bounces, pulls.
dot: A broken main leaf, or a quarter or more of the leaves broken, is out of service.
do: Replace spring pack ($400-1,200 installed).
:::
::: issue Shock absorber worn | watch
looks: Oil leaking down the shock body (wet, dirty), bent or broken shock, missing bushing.
feel: Bouncy ride, cupped tires.
do: Replace in pairs ($150-400 per axle).
:::
::: issue Torque rod bushing worn | soon
looks: Rubber in the torque rod ends cracked, pushed out, or metal-to-metal.
hear: Clunk when starting off and stopping.
feel: Drive axle walks, truck steers itself in a turn.
do: Replace rods ($200-600 each installed).
:::
::: issue U-bolt loose or broken | stop
looks: Rust streaks at the U-bolt nuts, shifted axle, broken bolt.
dot: Out of service.
do: Replace and re-torque. Loose U-bolts break springs and move the axle out of line.
:::
::: issue Spring hanger cracked | stop
looks: Crack in the cast hanger on the frame, rust line along the crack, hanger shifted.
do: Replace. A broken hanger drops the axle.
:::
::: issue Cab air bag or cab shock failed | watch
looks: Cab leans or bounces, cab bag flat.
feel: Rough ride in the cab.
do: Replace ($150-400).
:::
