# Engine core
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engine block | The main metal body holding the cylinders | Cracks, coolant or oil leaks | Replacement engine $25,000 to $40,000+ |
| Cylinder head | Bolted on top of the block; holds valves and injectors | Cracks, overheating damage, coolant loss | $5,000 to $12,000 |
| Head gasket | Seal between block and head | Coolant in oil, white smoke, overheating | $4,000 to $8,000 (head comes off) |
| Pistons and rings | Move up and down in the cylinders | Blow by, oil burning, low power | Part of an in frame overhaul, $20,000 to $40,000 |
| Cylinder liners | Replaceable sleeves the pistons run in | Scoring, coolant leaking into oil | Part of an overhaul |
| Connecting rods | Join pistons to the crankshaft | Knocking; can break through the block | Part of an overhaul |
| Crankshaft and main bearings | Turns piston motion into rotation | Knocking, low oil pressure | Part of an overhaul |
| Camshaft | Opens and closes the valves | Ticking, misfire, low power | $3,000 to $8,000 |
| Valves, springs, rocker arms | Let air in and exhaust out | Ticking, misfire; adjusted at overhead service | Valve adjustment $300 to $700 |
| Valve cover and gasket | Covers the top of the head | Oil leak on top of engine | $300 to $800 |
| Front and rear crankshaft seals | Seal oil at each end of the crank | Oil leak at front pulley or near the clutch | Front $400 to $900; rear $1,500 to $3,500 (transmission comes out) |
| Oil pan and gasket | Bottom of the engine, holds the oil | Oil leak, dented pan | $500 to $1,500 |
| Timing gears | Keep cam and crank in step | Rare failure, gear noise | $2,000 to $5,000 |
| Flywheel and flywheel housing | Heavy wheel at the back that the clutch presses on | Cracked or warped flywheel, replaced with clutch | $500 to $1,500 extra on a clutch job |
| Vibration damper | Round weight on the front of the crankshaft | Wobble, bulging, cracked | $800 to $1,800 |
| Engine mounts | Rubber mounts holding the engine to the frame | Excess vibration, engine movement | $300 to $800 each |
| Serpentine belt and tensioner | Drives alternator, water pump, fan and AC | Squeal, cracks, belt thrown | Belt $100 to $250; tensioner $250 to $600 |
| Idler pulleys | Guide the belt | Noise, wobble | $150 to $400 |
| Engine brake (Jake brake) | Uses engine compression to slow the truck | Not working, loud misfire | $1,000 to $3,000 |
| Crankcase breather / CCV filter | Vents crankcase gases | Oil from the breather, pressure codes | $150 to $500 |
