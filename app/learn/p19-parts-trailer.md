# Trailer
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
Trailers have their own brakes, tires, suspension, lights and annual inspection. The air brake, tire and light parts are the same as in the tables above; these are the parts only trailers have.

| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kingpin and upper coupler | Under the trailer front; locks into the fifth wheel | Worn kingpin, damaged plate | Kingpin $300 to $800; upper coupler $2,000 to $5,000 |
| Landing gear (legs) | Front legs holding the trailer up | Won't crank, bent leg | $800 to $2,000 per set |
| Landing gear crank handle and gearbox | Side of the trailer | Broken handle, slipping gears | $100 to $500 |
| Landing gear feet (sand shoes) | Bottom of the legs | Missing, broken | $50 to $200 |
| Trailer nose box (electrical junction) | Front of the trailer, where the 7 way plugs in | Corroded socket, lights out | $100 to $400 |
| Trailer gladhands and air lines | Front of the trailer | Leaks | $30 to $200 |
| Trailer air tank | Under the trailer | Leaks, water | $200 to $600 |
| Trailer relay / emergency valve | Controls trailer brakes | Brakes drag or won't apply | $150 to $400 |
| Tandem slider (sliding axle frame) | Lets the axles move forward and back | Stuck, pins won't engage | $300 to $1,500 |
| Slider locking pins and pull handle | Lock the slider in place | Pins not locked (dangerous), stuck | $100 to $400 |
| Trailer suspension (air ride or spring) | Axles | Air bag leaks, broken springs | Air bag $200 to $400; spring $300 to $800 |
| Trailer axle | Beam holding the wheels | Bent, damaged | $1,500 to $4,000 |
| Swing doors | Rear doors on a dry van or reefer | Won't close, bent | $1,000 to $3,000 per door |
| Door hinges, lock rods and cam keepers | Hold the doors | Broken hinge, bent rod | $100 to $500 |
| Door seals (gaskets) | Keep water and cold air out | Torn, leaking (reefer temperature issues) | $150 to $500 |
| Roll up door | Some trailers | Off track, broken spring | $300 to $1,500 |
| Floor (wood or aluminum) | Trailer floor | Holes, soft spots, forklift damage | Patch $300 to $1,000; section much more |
| Roof | Top of the box | Holes, leaks | Patch $300 to $1,500 |
| Side panels and scuff liner | Walls | Holes, dents | $300 to $2,000 |
| Logistic posts, E track, load bars / straps | Securing freight inside | Broken, missing | $30 to $200 |
| Rear underride guard (ICC bumper) | Back of the trailer, protects cars | Bent, damaged (violation) | $500 to $1,500 |
| Side skirts (trailer) | Aero panels under the trailer | Cracked, hanging | $300 to $1,000 each |
| Trailer tail (rear fairing) | Folding panels on the back | Broken, won't fold | $300 to $1,500 |
| Mud flaps and hangers | Behind trailer tires | Missing | $20 to $100 |
| Reflective tape and ID lights | Sides and rear | Missing (violation) | $100 to $400 |
| Registration holder and annual inspection sticker | Front of the trailer | Missing paperwork | Inspection $100 to $250 |
