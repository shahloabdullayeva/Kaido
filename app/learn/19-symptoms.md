# Symptoms and likely causes
When a driver describes a problem, these are the usual suspects. Always confirm with codes and a mechanic.

## Engine and power
| Driver says | Usual causes |
|---|---|
| Low power, won't go over 55 or 5 mph | Aftertreatment derate (DPF, DEF, NOx sensor), boost leak, fuel filter |
| Truck won't start, cranks | Out of fuel or air in fuel, gelled fuel, fuel filter, injector or rail pressure fault |
| Truck won't crank, just clicks | Batteries, cables, starter, starter relay |
| Black smoke | Boost leak, turbo, air filter, injector |
| White smoke that does not clear | Coolant burning (EGR cooler, head gasket), injector |
| Blue smoke | Oil burning: turbo seals, rings |
| Engine shut down by itself | Low oil pressure, high coolant temperature, low coolant level (the engine protection system) |
| Knocking noise | Injector, bearing, serious internal damage. Stop and do not drive |
| Overheating | Low coolant, fan clutch, thermostat, water pump, clogged radiator, burst hose |
| Coolant level keeps dropping, no leak seen | EGR cooler, head gasket, oil cooler |
| Oil on the ground | Oil pan gasket, filter, turbo line, rear main seal |
| Fuel smell | Fuel line, filter housing, tank leak |

## Air and brakes
| Driver says | Usual causes |
|---|---|
| Air leak (hissing) | Lines, fittings, brake chamber, gladhand seal, valve |
| Air takes too long to build | Compressor, air dryer, leak, governor |
| Truck pulls when braking | Brake out of adjustment on one side, contaminated lining, a chamber not working |
| Brakes dragging, hot wheel | Spring brake not fully released, broken return spring, bad chamber |
| ABS lamp | Wheel speed sensor, wiring, ABS module, 7-way plug (trailer ABS) |

## Drivetrain
| Driver says | Usual causes |
|---|---|
| Won't go into gear (automated) | Low air, clutch calibration needed, sensor, shifter |
| Vibration at highway speed | Driveshaft, U-joint, carrier bearing, out-of-balance tire |
| Clunk when starting or stopping | U-joint, fifth wheel, torque rods, suspension bushings |
| Grinding from the rear axle | Differential, wheel bearing |

## Tires, steering and suspension
| Driver says | Usual causes |
|---|---|
| Pulls to one side | Alignment, tire pressure, brake drag, worn steering parts |
| Steering loose or wandering | Kingpins, tie rod ends, steering box |
| Truck leaning | Air bag leak, height control valve, broken spring |
| Uneven tire wear | Alignment, pressure, worn suspension |

## Electrical
| Driver says | Usual causes |
|---|---|
| Lights flicker on the trailer | 7-way plug corrosion, trailer wiring |
| Battery lamp, dim lights | Alternator, belt, wiring |
| Dash gauges dead or crazy | Data network fault, ground wire, instrument cluster |

## Cab and HVAC
| Driver says | Usual causes |
|---|---|
| No AC | Low refrigerant (leak), compressor clutch, pressure switch, blower |
| No heat | Low coolant, thermostat, blend door, heater core |
| Blower works only on high | Blower resistor |
| APU not running | APU fuel, APU battery, belts, service overdue |
