# Wheel end issues
Hubs, bearings, seals, lug nuts and wheels: where wheel-offs and brake fires start.
::: issue Wheel seal leaking | soon
looks: Oil sprayed on the inside of the wheel and tire, a wet ring around the hub, dirt stuck to oil. Hubcap window empty or cloudy.
why: Seal worn, bearing loose, overfilled hub, vent plugged.
do: Replace seal ($200-500 per wheel end). Check brake linings for oil.
dot: Leaking seal with oil on the brakes is out of service.
:::
::: issue Hub oil low | soon
looks: Oil level below the line in the clear hubcap window, or no oil visible.
do: Fill with the right oil (gear oil for most oil-bath hubs). Find the leak.
:::
::: issue Wheel bearing failing | stop
hear: Growl, roar or grinding from one wheel, louder in turns.
looks: Hub hot, smoke from the hub, wheel wobbles when lifted, metal flakes in the hub oil.
do: Stop. A failed bearing can lock up or let the wheel come off. Road service.
cost: Bearing and seal $400-900 per wheel; with hub damage more.
:::
::: issue Loose wheel (lug nuts) | stop
looks: Rust streaks running from lug nuts (rust trail), shiny metal around a nut, elongated stud holes, nut indicators (plastic arrows) pointing different ways, missing nut.
hear: Clunking or clicking from the wheel.
do: Stop now. Re-torque only after checking the studs and holes. Wheel-offs kill people.
dot: Loose or missing lug nut is a violation; two or more missing or loose on a wheel is out of service.
:::
::: issue Broken wheel stud | stop
looks: Stud broken off flush, nut missing.
do: Replace the stud and check the others. Torque to spec (usually 450-500 ft-lb on hub-piloted wheels).
:::
::: issue Cracked wheel | stop
looks: Crack between stud holes, from a hand hole or at the rim edge. Rust line along a crack.
do: Replace wheel. Out of service.
:::
::: issue Hub overheated | stop
looks: Paint on the hub burned or discolored, smell of hot oil, hubcap melted.
do: Stop and cool. Inspect bearing and brakes.
:::
::: issue Wheel bearing end play too much | soon
check: With the wheel off the ground, rock it top to bottom. Movement more than about 0.005" with a dial gauge, or felt by hand, means the bearing is out of adjustment.
do: Re-adjust or replace. Also pushes the ABS sensor away (ABS lamp).
:::
