# Clutch and transmission
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clutch (pressure plate and discs) | Between engine and transmission | Slipping, grabbing, won't disengage | $2,000 to $4,500 |
| Release bearing (throwout bearing) | Releases the clutch | Squeal when pedal pressed | Done with clutch |
| Clutch brake | Stops the gearbox so first gear goes in | Grinding into first | Done with clutch, $200 to $500 alone |
| Clutch actuator (AMT) | Computer controlled clutch operation | Won't engage, AMT faults | $800 to $2,500 |
| Clutch linkage / hydraulic cylinder | Pedal to clutch on manuals | Soft pedal, leaks | $200 to $800 |
| Transmission (manual or AMT) | Gearbox | Won't shift, noise, leaks | Internal repair $1,500 to $5,000; rebuild $5,000 to $8,500; reman $6,000 to $12,000 |
| Gear set | Gears inside the transmission | Grinding, jumping out of gear | Part of a rebuild |
| Shift control module / gear selector | AMT shifter and electronics | Shift faults, no gear | $500 to $2,000 |
| Shift lever, knob and air lines (manual) | Range and splitter switches on the knob | Won't change range | $100 to $400 |
| Transmission cooler | Cools transmission oil | Leaks | $400 to $1,200 |
| Output shaft and yoke | Where the driveshaft attaches | Leaking seal, play | Seal $200 to $500 |
| Transmission seals | Front and rear seals | Oil leaks | $200 to $1,500 |
| Transmission oil service | Drain and refill | Done every few hundred thousand miles | $250 to $500 |
