# Lights issues
Headlights, marker lights, brake lights, turn signals and trailer lights.
::: issue Headlight out or dim | soon
looks: One headlight off, yellowed lens, low beam pointing wrong.
dot: Headlight out at night is a violation.
do: Replace bulb or LED unit ($30-400).
:::
::: issue Brake light out | soon
looks: One or both stop lights do not come on when the brake is applied. Check with a helper or the reflection in a window.
dot: Both stop lights out is out of service; one is a violation.
do: Replace bulb or LED.
:::
::: issue Turn signal flashing fast | soon
looks: Turn signal on the dash blinks much faster than normal.
why: A bulb out on that side (tractor or trailer).
do: Find and replace.
:::
::: issue Trailer lights all out or flickering | soon
looks: No trailer lights, or lights flicker over bumps.
why: 7-way cord or plug, broken ground wire, corroded pin, trailer harness chafed.
do: Check the cord at both ends first. Clean pins.
:::
::: issue Marker or clearance light out | watch
looks: One amber or red marker light dark, lens cracked or missing.
do: Replace ($15-60 each). A common roadside violation.
:::
::: issue Reflective tape missing | watch
looks: Red and white conspicuity tape faded, peeled or missing on the trailer sides and rear.
do: Replace tape. Required on trailers.
:::
::: issue Lens cracked or water inside | watch
looks: Cracked lens, fog or water inside a light.
do: Replace. Water kills LEDs and sockets.
:::
