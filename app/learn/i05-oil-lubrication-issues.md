# Oil and lubrication issues
Oil pressure, leaks, oil condition and grease points.
::: issue Low oil pressure | stop
dash: Oil pressure gauge low (under 10-15 psi at idle), red oil lamp, stop engine.
why: Low oil level, oil pump, worn bearings, clogged pickup, fuel-diluted oil, failing sensor.
check: Dipstick first. If the level is fine, a mechanic checks with a mechanical gauge before tearing down.
do: Stop now. Sensor faults happen, but never assume.
:::
::: issue Oil level low | soon
looks: Dipstick below the low mark (checked cold, truck level, 15 minutes after shut down).
why: Leak, burning (blue smoke), or just not checked.
do: Top up with the right grade (most modern diesels: 10W-30 or 15W-40 CK-4/FA-4 per the engine). Find where it goes.
:::
::: issue Oil level rising | soon
looks: Dipstick above the full mark without adding oil.
why: Fuel or coolant getting into the oil.
do: Oil sample. See fuel in oil and coolant in oil in [Engine issues](/learn/engine-issues).
:::
::: issue Oil pan or gasket leak | watch
looks: Oil dripping from the bottom of the engine, wet pan rail, dirt caked in oil.
do: Plan repair. Pan gasket $400-1,200.
:::
::: issue Rear main seal leak | watch
looks: Oil dripping between the engine and transmission (bell housing), oil slung around the clutch housing.
why: Seal worn. Can soak the clutch and cause it to slip.
do: Repair at next clutch job or soon if heavy ($1,500-3,500 because the transmission comes out).
:::
::: issue Oil filter leak | soon
looks: Fresh oil around the filter after a service, filter loose, double gasket (old gasket stuck on the base).
do: Check right away. A double gasket can blow out and dump all the oil in minutes.
:::
::: issue Oil sample shows wear metals or silicon | soon
looks: Lab report flags iron, copper, lead, aluminum (wear) or silicon (dirt).
why: Silicon means dirt getting past the air filter. Copper from oil cooler. Lead from bearings.
do: Follow the lab's notes. Inspect the intake for cracks.
:::
::: issue Greasing missed | watch
looks: Dry, rusty kingpins and U-joints; no fresh grease at the fittings; squeaks over bumps.
why: Lube interval skipped. Kingpins, slack adjusters, U-joints, fifth wheel, spring pins need grease every PM.
do: Grease at next stop. A dry kingpin or U-joint wears out fast.
:::
::: issue Oil change overdue | watch
looks: Oil black and thin, past the miles on the sticker or in Kaido service due.
do: Change. Modern intervals 25,000-60,000 miles depending on engine, oil and duty cycle.
cost: $250-450 at a truck stop service bay.
:::
