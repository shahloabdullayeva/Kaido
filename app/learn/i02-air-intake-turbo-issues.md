# Air intake and turbo issues
Air filters, charge air coolers, boost leaks and turbos: what failure looks and sounds like.
::: issue Air filter restricted | watch
looks: Restriction indicator on the filter housing or dash shows red or a high number (over 20-25 inches of water). Filter element grey-black, packed with dust or soaked.
feel: Slow power loss over weeks, fuel economy down.
do: Replace the element. Do not tap it clean.
cost: $60-150 parts, 15 minutes.
:::
::: issue Wet or crushed air filter | soon
looks: Filter element wet, swollen, or the paper folds collapsed. Water stains in the housing.
why: Rain or snow entering a cracked intake or missing cap, pressure washing the engine.
do: Replace now. A collapsed filter can let dirt in and "dust" the engine, which wears the rings in weeks.
:::
::: issue Cracked intake piping (dirt getting in) | soon
looks: Cracked rubber elbow or loose clamp between the air filter and the turbo. Dust trail on the inside of the pipe after the filter.
why: Age, heat, poor clamping.
do: Replace the piping. Dirt past the filter means engine wear.
:::
::: issue Boost leak at the charge air cooler or boots | soon
looks: Oily film and dirt stuck around a hose joint or a seam on the charge air cooler (the radiator in front of the radiator). Split rubber boot, clamp loose.
hear: Whistle or hiss that rises with throttle.
feel: Low power, black smoke, higher fuel use.
dash: Sometimes codes for low boost or EGR flow.
check: Pressure test the charge air system (shops do it in 30 minutes).
do: Replace boots and clamps ($100-300) or the charge air cooler ($900-2,000 installed).
:::
::: issue Turbo whine or howl | soon
hear: High-pitched siren or whine that rises and falls with throttle, louder than usual.
looks: Oil wetness at the turbo outlet or in the boost piping.
why: Worn turbo bearings, shaft play, damaged compressor wheel.
check: With the engine off and the pipe removed, the shaft should spin freely and not touch the housing. Small side play is normal; touching is not.
do: Shop. Turbo $2,000-4,500 installed.
:::
::: issue Turbo failed | stop
looks: Heavy blue or black smoke, oil pouring from the stack, oil in the intake piping. Broken pieces of the wheel in the piping.
hear: Grinding or screaming, then a sudden drop in power.
dash: Low boost codes, VGT actuator codes.
do: Stop. Broken turbo pieces or oil can run the engine away (engine running on its own oil). Tow.
:::
::: issue VGT actuator fault | soon
dash: Codes for the variable geometry turbo actuator (Cummins SPN 641, Detroit and Volvo similar). Check engine lamp.
feel: Low power, or too much boost on engine brake.
why: Electronic actuator failure, sticky vanes from soot.
do: Shop. Actuator $800-2,000; sometimes a cleaning and calibration fixes it.
:::
::: issue Exhaust manifold leak | watch
looks: Black soot streaks on the manifold or turbo flange, broken studs.
hear: Ticking or puffing at cold start that fades as it warms.
smell: Exhaust in the cab.
do: Plan repair: gaskets and studs $500-1,500. Cracked manifold $1,000-2,500.
:::
::: issue EGR valve stuck | soon
dash: EGR flow codes, check engine.
looks: Black smoke, rough running, soot in the intake.
why: Soot build-up, failed actuator.
do: Clean or replace. $800-2,500 depending on engine.
:::
