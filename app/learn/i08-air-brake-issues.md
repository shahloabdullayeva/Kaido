# Air brake issues
Air leaks, compressors, dryers, slack adjusters, chambers, linings and drums.
## Air supply
::: issue Air leak | soon
hear: Steady hiss. Louder with brakes applied (service side) or released (supply side).
looks: Soapy water bubbles at the leak. A frosted spot on a fitting in cold weather.
check: Leak-down test: engine off, brakes released, pressure should drop less than 2-3 psi per minute; with brakes applied less than 3-4 psi per minute.
why: Line rubbed through, loose fitting, chamber diaphragm, gladhand seal, valve, air bag.
do: Big leaks: stop. Small leaks: shop today.
dot: Audible leak at a chamber, or leak-down over the limit, is out of service.
:::
::: issue Air builds slowly | soon
looks: Takes more than 45 seconds to go from 85 to 100 psi. Low air warning stays on longer at start-up.
why: Compressor worn, intake to compressor restricted, governor fault, big leak, dryer plugged.
do: Shop. Compressor $1,200-3,000 installed.
:::
::: issue Compressor pumping oil | soon
looks: Oil when draining the air tanks (more than a teaspoon), oily air dryer cartridge, oil at the gladhands.
why: Worn compressor. Normal is a small amount.
do: Replace the dryer cartridge, check the compressor. Oil ruins valves over time.
:::
::: issue Air dryer purging constantly or not at all | soon
hear: Dryer pops every few seconds, or never purges.
looks: Water in the tanks when drained (dryer not working).
do: Replace dryer cartridge ($100-250) or purge valve. Drain tanks daily in winter.
:::
::: issue Frozen air lines | soon
looks: Brakes will not release, slow to build air in freezing weather, ice in the gladhands.
why: Water in the air from a failed dryer.
do: Thaw (heat, air line antifreeze only if the system allows it). Fix the dryer.
:::
::: issue Governor out of setting | soon
check: Cut-out over 135 psi or under 100 psi, cut-in too low.
do: Adjust or replace ($100-300).
:::
## Foundation brakes
::: issue Brakes out of adjustment | soon
looks: Pushrod travels more than the limit when the brakes are applied (marked by paint stripe or a stroke indicator on the pushrod).
feel: Long pedal, weak braking, pull to one side.
why: Automatic slack adjuster worn, seized or installed wrong. Automatic slacks should never be adjusted by hand to fix stroke; that hides the real problem.
do: Shop: find why the automatic slack is not adjusting. Slack adjuster $150-400 installed.
dot: The number one brake violation. 20% or more of brakes out of adjustment puts the vehicle out of service.
:::
::: issue Brake lining worn | soon
looks: Lining thinner than 1/4" at the thinnest spot (drum brakes) or under 1/8" (disc pads). Wear indicator groove gone. Rivet heads near the surface.
hear: Grinding or scraping metal when braking.
do: Reline that axle ($400-900 per axle with drums).
dot: Lining under the limit is out of service.
:::
::: issue Brake lining soaked with oil or grease | soon
looks: Dark, shiny, wet look on the lining; oil streaks on the inside of the wheel, drum and backing plate.
why: Leaking wheel seal.
do: Replace the wheel seal and the linings on both sides of the axle. Contaminated linings do not dry out.
dot: Contaminated lining is out of service.
:::
::: issue Cracked or broken brake drum | stop
looks: Crack running across the drum surface from the open edge, or a piece missing. Blue heat marks and checking (fine cracks) on the inside.
do: Replace drum ($150-400 plus labor). A cracked drum can break apart.
dot: A crack across the whole width, or a piece missing, is out of service.
:::
::: issue Brake chamber leaking or damaged | soon
hear: Hiss at the chamber, louder when the brakes are applied.
looks: Dented or rusted chamber, broken clamp ring, pushrod out at an angle.
do: Replace chamber ($150-350 installed).
:::
::: issue Spring brake caged or broken spring | stop
looks: Caging bolt left in, spring chamber rusted through, cracked housing.
do: Replace chamber. A broken parking spring can release with great force: only a mechanic should handle it.
:::
::: issue Dragging brake, hot wheel | soon
looks: One wheel much hotter than the others (feel carefully with the back of the hand near, not on, the hub), smoke or burning smell, brake dust on that wheel.
why: Spring brake not fully releasing, broken return spring, seized cam, relay valve.
do: Stop and let it cool. Shop. Dragging brakes cause fires and blown tires.
:::
::: issue ABS lamp on | soon
dash: Tractor ABS lamp on, or trailer ABS lamp on the dash or on the trailer's side.
why: Wheel speed sensor gap (most common: sensor pushed back by a loose bearing), wiring, 7-way plug, ABS module.
do: Shop with ABS reader. Sensor push-in fixes many ($50-250).
dot: ABS lamp on is a violation (not out of service).
:::
::: issue Gladhand seal leaking | watch
hear: Hiss at the trailer air connection.
looks: Seal cracked, flattened or missing.
do: Replace seal (a few dollars, 1 minute). Drivers should carry spares.
:::
::: issue Air line rubbing or kinked | soon
looks: Air hose between tractor and trailer dragging on the deck plate or frame, worn shiny spot, kinked.
do: Fix hose support (spring or tender). Replace worn hoses.
:::
