# Electrical, electronics and sensors
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Batteries (3 to 4) | Battery box under the cab step | No start, slow crank, dead after parking | $100 to $200 each; set $300 to $800 |
| Battery cables and terminals | Connect batteries, starter, ground | Corrosion, melted cable | $100 to $400 |
| Battery disconnect switch | Cuts all power | Truck dead with good batteries | $50 to $200 |
| Alternator | Belt driven, charges batteries | Battery light, low voltage | $300 to $700 |
| Starter motor | Cranks the engine | Click, no crank | $400 to $900 |
| Starter relay / solenoid | Switches the starter | Click only | $50 to $200 |
| ECM (engine control module) | Engine computer | No start, no communication | $1,500 to $3,500 plus programming |
| TCM (transmission control module) | AMT computer | Shift faults | $1,000 to $3,000 |
| ACM (aftertreatment control module) | Emissions computer | Emissions codes | $1,000 to $2,500 |
| SAM cab / SAM chassis (Freightliner) | Modules controlling lights, wipers and accessories | Random electrical faults | $800 to $2,000 each |
| ABS module | Brake computer | ABS light | $500 to $1,500 |
| Instrument cluster (dash) | Gauges and warning lights | Dead gauges, blank screen | $800 to $2,500 |
| Fuses and relays | Fuse box in the cab and under the hood | One item stops working | $5 to $50 each part |
| Wiring harness and connectors | All the wiring | Chafed wires, corrosion, intermittent faults | Diagnosis $150 to $500; repair $500 to $2,500 |
| 7 way trailer cord and socket | Power to trailer lights and ABS | Trailer lights out | Cord $50 to $150; socket $50 to $150 |
| Horn (electric and air) | Horn | Not working | $50 to $200 |
| Wiper motor and arms | Windshield wipers | Stuck, slow | $300 to $700 |
| Power window motor / regulator | Door windows | Window stuck | $200 to $500 |
| Door lock actuator | Power locks | Won't lock or unlock | $150 to $400 |
| Crankshaft and camshaft position sensors | Engine timing for the ECM | Stall, no start | $150 to $400 |
| Vehicle speed sensor | Speedometer and cruise | Speedo dead, shifting issues | $100 to $300 |
| Oil, coolant, fuel, boost, temperature sensors | Many small sensors feeding the ECM | Codes, derate | $100 to $500 each |
| ELD / telematics unit | Logs hours, sends location and codes | Malfunction, driver can't log | Usually replaced by the provider |
| Dash camera | Road and driver facing cameras | Offline | Usually by the provider |
