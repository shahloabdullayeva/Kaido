# Cab and body issues
Windshields, mirrors, wipers, doors, seats and body damage.
::: issue Windshield cracked | soon
looks: Crack in the driver's view (the area swept by the wipers), a star chip larger than 3/4", or two chips close together.
dot: Cracks in the swept area are a violation; small chips outside it are allowed.
do: Replace ($400-1,200 installed) or repair chips early ($50-120).
:::
::: issue Wipers not working | soon
looks: Wipers do not move, move slow, or blades streak and chatter. Washer does not spray.
why: Blades worn, wiper motor, linkage, fuse, frozen washer fluid.
do: Blades $30-60; motor $300-700.
dot: Wipers not working is out of service in rain or snow.
:::
::: issue Mirror broken or loose | soon
looks: Mirror glass cracked, mirror arm loose and shaking, heated mirror not clearing.
do: Replace glass ($50-150) or arm ($200-600).
:::
::: issue Door will not latch | soon
looks: Door opens on bumps, latch does not catch, gap at the door edge.
do: Adjust or replace latch.
:::
::: issue Seat air leak or broken seat | watch
looks: Air seat does not hold height, hiss under the seat, broken seat belt latch.
do: Repair; seat belt must work.
:::
::: issue Body damage | watch
looks: Fairing cracked or missing, bumper bent, hood cracked, fender torn. Photos with the unit number for the work order.
do: Body shop. Check for frame or radiator damage behind front-end damage.
:::
::: issue Horn not working | watch
looks: Electric or air horn silent.
dot: Horn required.
do: Fuse, switch, horn.
:::
::: issue Fire extinguisher or triangles missing | watch
looks: Extinguisher missing, not charged (needle in red), no three warning triangles.
dot: Required equipment. Violation.
do: Replace ($40-80).
:::
