# Wheel ends and tires
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steer tire | Front axle, straight ribs | Cuts, uneven wear, below 4/32" | $350 to $700 new |
| Drive tire | Drive axles, blocky lugs | Below 2/32", cuts, irregular wear | $300 to $600 new |
| Trailer tire | Trailer axles, ribs | Below 2/32", flats | $250 to $450 new |
| Retread tire | Drive or trailer | Tread separation | $150 to $250 |
| Used tire (Grade A) | Any non steer position | Age, hidden repairs | $150 to $300 |
| Wide base single (super single) | Replaces a pair of duals | Same checks | $500 to $900 |
| Tire repair (plug / patch) | Nail or puncture | Slow leak | $30 to $50 in shop; more roadside |
| Mount, dismount and balance | Putting a tire on a wheel | Vibration if unbalanced | $30 to $80 per tire (est.) |
| Valve stem and extensions | Where air goes in; extensions on inner duals | Slow leak | $10 to $40 |
| Wheel (steel) | Rim | Cracks, bent, bad welds | $150 to $300 |
| Wheel (aluminum) | Lighter, shiny rim | Cracks, curb damage | $300 to $600 |
| Lug nuts and wheel studs | 10 per wheel | Missing nut, rust trails, broken stud | Nut $5 to $15; stud replacement $50 to $150 |
| Hub | Wheel mounts to it | Damage, bearing failure | $400 to $1,200 |
| Wheel bearings | Inside the hub | Noise, heat, wheel wobble | $300 to $800 per wheel |
| Wheel seal | Keeps oil in the hub | Oil on wheel and brakes (violation) | $200 to $500 per wheel |
| Steer hub cap (oil cap with window) | Center of front wheel | Cracked, low oil level | $20 to $60 part |
| Hub cover / lug nut covers | Cosmetic covers | Missing, broken | $10 to $40 |
| Tire pressure monitoring (TPMS) | Sensors on each tire | Warning light | $50 to $150 per sensor |
| Automatic tire inflation (trailers) | Keeps trailer tires aired | Hose leaks, light on | $100 to $400 repairs |
| Spare tire (rarely carried) | Most trucks rely on road service | None | n/a |
