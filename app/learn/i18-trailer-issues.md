# Trailer issues
Dry vans, reefers and flatbeds: landing gear, sliders, doors, floors, roofs and trailer brakes.
::: issue Tandem slider pins not locked | stop
looks: Slider locking pins not fully through the holes in the rail; release handle out.
hear: Bang when braking or starting as the tandem slides.
do: Lock before moving. A loose slider can slide out of the rails.
:::
::: issue Landing gear will not crank or is bent | soon
looks: Leg bent, crank handle missing, gear box grinding, foot pad broken.
do: Repair ($300-1,200 per side). Cannot drop the trailer safely otherwise.
:::
::: issue Trailer floor damaged | soon
looks: Holes, broken boards, soft spots, forklift tracks through the floor.
do: Floor repair ($300 to several thousand). Shippers reject damaged trailers.
:::
::: issue Roof leak or hole | soon
looks: Daylight through the roof from inside, water stains on the walls or cargo.
do: Patch ($150-600). Leaks cause cargo claims.
:::
::: issue Swing doors or hinges damaged | soon
looks: Door will not close tight, broken hinge, cam lock bent, seal torn, door rod bent.
do: Repair hinges and seals. Unsecured doors open on the road.
:::
::: issue Trailer brakes dragging | soon
looks: Trailer wheel hot, brake smell, trailer pulling back on the tractor.
why: Spring brakes not releasing (low supply air, gladhand mixed up), relay valve, broken spring.
do: Check gladhands are on the right lines (service to service, emergency to emergency). Shop if still dragging.
:::
::: issue Trailer air leak | soon
hear: Hiss at the trailer, louder with brakes applied.
why: Gladhand seals, lines, chamber, relay valve, air tank drain left open.
do: Find and fix.
:::
::: issue Trailer ABS fault | soon
looks: Trailer ABS lamp on the trailer's left side stays on after power-up (it should come on and go out), or the dash trailer ABS lamp.
why: Sensor, wiring, 7-way power (blue wire), module.
do: Shop.
:::
::: issue Trailer tire flat or missing | stop
looks: One tire of a dual flat or shredded, rim on the ground.
do: Road service. The good tire next to it is overloaded and will blow too.
:::
::: issue Trailer registration or annual inspection expired | watch
looks: Annual inspection sticker over 12 months old, registration out of date.
dot: Violation.
do: Inspection at the next PM. Kaido Documents tracks the dates.
:::
::: issue Flatbed securement damaged | soon
looks: Straps cut or frayed, chains with stretched links, winches bent, stake pockets broken.
dot: Damaged tie-downs are out of service.
do: Replace.
:::
