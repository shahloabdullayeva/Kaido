# Lights
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
Lights are the number one roadside violation, so they come up constantly. Most modern truck lights are LED.

| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headlight assembly | Front | Out, cracked lens, moisture inside | $200 to $600 each |
| Headlight bulb | Older trucks | Burned out | $20 to $60 |
| Fog lights | Bumper | Out | $50 to $200 |
| Turn signals (front, side, rear) | All corners | Out, fast blinking | $20 to $100 each |
| Clearance and marker lights (amber front, red rear) | Cab roof, sides, trailer | Out (very common violation) | $10 to $50 each part |
| Cab roof ID lights (three amber) | Top of the cab | Out | $10 to $40 each |
| Tail, stop and turn lights | Rear of tractor and trailer | Out | $20 to $80 each |
| Rear ID lights (three red) | Rear of the trailer | Out | $10 to $40 each |
| License plate light | Rear | Out | $10 to $40 |
| Backup lights | Rear | Out | $20 to $60 |
| Flasher relay | Controls turn signals | Hyperflash or no flash | $20 to $80 |
| Light wiring and pigtails | Connections to each light | Corrosion, intermittent lights | $50 to $300 |
| Reflective tape (conspicuity tape) | Trailer sides and rear | Missing or faded (violation) | $100 to $400 |
