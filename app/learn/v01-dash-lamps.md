# Dash lamps
Every common warning light on a Class 8 dash, drawn, with what it means and what the driver should do.
## How to read the colors
- **Red** means stop or act now. Something can hurt the engine or the driver. Pull over safely.
- **Amber (yellow)** means a fault was found. The truck can usually keep going, but it needs attention soon.
- **Green and blue** are information only: a system is on (cruise, engine brake, high beams).
- **Flashing** is always more urgent than solid. A flashing amber lamp is close to becoming a red one.
> tip: The icons below are drawn to look like the common ones. Each brand (Freightliner, Volvo, Kenworth, Peterbilt, International, Mack) draws them a little differently, and newer trucks show a text message on the center screen too. Ask the driver to read the message word for word.
> warn: Two lamps at once tell a story. Check engine plus DPF plus DEF usually means an aftertreatment problem that is heading to a derate. Red stop plus oil can means shut down now.
## Red lamps: stop
| Lamp | Name | What it means | What to tell the driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| {{lamp:stop-engine}} | Stop engine | A serious engine fault: low oil pressure, high coolant temperature, low coolant level, high intake temperature. Most engines start a 30 second shutdown timer | Pull over at once where safe. Note any message on the screen. Do not restart until the cause is known |
| {{lamp:oil-pressure}} | Low oil pressure | Oil pressure under the safe limit, often under 10-15 psi at idle | Shut down now. Check the dipstick. Running without pressure destroys bearings in minutes |
| {{lamp:coolant-temp}} | High coolant temperature | Coolant above about 220-230 °F | Pull over, idle a minute with the fan on if it is not boiling over, then shut down. Never open the radiator cap hot |
| {{lamp:coolant-level}} | Low coolant level | Coolant tank below the sensor | Stop and check level cold. Look for a leak or white smoke. Usually followed by a derate or shutdown |
| {{lamp:low-air}} | Low air pressure | Air in a brake tank below about 60 psi. A buzzer sounds too | Stop safely now. Below 20-45 psi the spring brakes apply on their own |
| {{lamp:brake-system}} | Brake system | A brake fault or parking brake on | Check the parking brake knob; if released and the lamp stays, stop and call in |
| {{lamp:parking-brake}} | Parking brake | Parking brakes are applied | Normal when parked. If it is on while driving, stop: a brake is dragging |
| {{lamp:battery}} | Charging | Alternator not charging, or voltage too high or too low | Turn off extra loads, check the belt, get to a shop before the batteries die |
| {{lamp:seat-belt}} | Seat belt | Driver belt not buckled | Buckle. Shows on safety reports |
| {{lamp:door-ajar}} | Door open | A cab door or the hood is not latched | Close it before driving |
## Amber lamps: fix soon
| Lamp | Name | What it means | What to tell the driver |
|---|---|---|---|
| {{lamp:check-engine}} | Check engine | The engine computer logged a fault. Can be anything from a sensor to a coolant problem | Read the code (SPN and FMI) from the dash or Samsara. Keep driving carefully and plan a shop |
| {{lamp:mil}} | MIL (malfunction indicator) | An emissions fault. Solid: logged. Flashing: serious emissions fault | Get the code. Often comes with DPF or DEF lamps. Can turn into a derate within hours |
| {{lamp:dpf}} | DPF | The diesel particulate filter is filling with soot. Solid: regen needed. Flashing: regen needed now | Do a parked regen as soon as safe (20-60 minutes). If it is flashing with check engine, power will be cut soon |
| {{lamp:dpf-regen-off}} | Regen disabled | The driver or a fault turned regeneration off | Make sure the regen switch is not set to inhibit. Turn it back on unless parked somewhere unsafe |
| {{lamp:hest}} | HEST (high exhaust temperature) | A regen is running and the exhaust is very hot (over 900 °F) | Normal during regen. Do not park over grass, paper or fuel spills |
| {{lamp:def-low}} | DEF | DEF tank low, or DEF quality or system fault | Fill DEF. If full and the lamp stays, it is a DEF system fault: derate follows in hours |
| {{lamp:abs}} | ABS (tractor) | Anti-lock system fault. Normal brakes still work | Get it checked soon. A lit ABS lamp is a roadside violation |
| {{lamp:trailer-abs}} | Trailer ABS | Trailer ABS fault, often power through the 7-way cord | Check the 7-way plug and cord. Lamp should flash on and off when the trailer is powered up |
| {{lamp:water-in-fuel}} | Water in fuel | Water collected in the fuel water separator | Drain the separator at the next stop. If it keeps coming back, the fuel or a tank is contaminated |
| {{lamp:low-fuel}} | Low fuel | Fuel down to the last few percent | Fuel now. Running dry lets air into the system and the truck may not restart without priming |
| {{lamp:wait-to-start}} | Wait to start / intake heater | Intake grid heater warming the air in the cold | Wait until it goes out before cranking |
| {{lamp:transmission}} | Transmission | Automated transmission fault, or it needs a clutch calibration | Get the code. Often low air or a clutch that needs calibration. Truck may stay in a limp gear |
| {{lamp:tpms}} | Tire pressure | A tire is low (only on trucks with tire sensors) | Check pressures with a gauge at the next stop |
| {{lamp:collision}} | Collision mitigation | Radar system (Detroit Assurance, Bendix Wingman, Volvo Active Driver Assist) fault or blocked | Clean the radar on the bumper. If it stays on, the system is off and needs a shop |
| {{lamp:lane-departure}} | Lane departure | Lane camera fault or blocked windshield | Clean the windshield in front of the camera |
| {{lamp:service}} | Service due | Maintenance reminder or general vehicle message | Read the center screen message |
## Green and blue: information
| Lamp | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| {{lamp:engine-brake}} | Engine brake (Jake) | Engine brake switched on |
| {{lamp:cruise}} | Cruise | Cruise control set |
| {{lamp:high-beam}} | High beams | High beams on |

## Lamp combinations and what they usually mean
| Lamps on together | Most likely story | Urgency |
|---|---|---|
| Check engine + DPF solid | Soot building, regen needed | Regen today |
| Check engine + DPF flashing + HEST | Regen tried and failed or is running hot | Parked regen now, shop if it fails |
| Check engine + DEF + MIL | DEF quality, DEF pump, doser or NOx sensor fault | Derate in hours. Shop today |
| Stop engine + coolant level | Coolant leak or burning coolant | Stop. Do not drive |
| Stop engine + oil | Low oil or oil pump problem | Stop. Do not restart |
| Check engine + charging | Alternator or belt | Shop before the truck dies |
| ABS + trailer ABS | 7-way cord or plug | Check cord, then shop |
| Low air + buzzer while driving | Big air leak or compressor failure | Stop now |
