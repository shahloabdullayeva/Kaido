# Study schedule
Three weeks at about two hours a day. Week 1 alone is enough to handle a breakdown call.

## How to use it
Each day names the topics to read (from Fleet From Scratch and Parts A to Z) and one thing to do so it sticks.

## Week 1: the job and the big four
1. **Day 1.** Read Start here, How fleets work, The truck. Do: draw the 18 tire positions from memory and label LF, RF, LRO, RRI.
2. **Day 2.** Read Reading a VIN and a unit profile, Diesel engine basics. Do: decode three VINs with the NHTSA decoder and name brand, year and engine.
3. **Day 3.** Read Air intake, turbo and emissions, Fault codes you'll see, Parts A to Z: Exhaust and emissions. Do: explain regen and derate out loud in two sentences each; learn SPN 3719, 3251, 3216, 3226, 4364, 5246.
4. **Day 4.** Read Air brakes, Parts A to Z: Air brakes. Do: explain why brakes out of adjustment are the top DOT violation, and what an air leak call sounds like.
5. **Day 5.** Read Tires and wheels, Parts A to Z: Wheel ends and tires. Do: legal tread (4/32" steer, 2/32" drive and trailer), and compare tire prices you have seen with the table.
6. **Day 6.** Read Breakdowns and road service. Do: write the driver intake questions from memory, then a first mock call with a friend.
7. **Day 7.** Review. Do: flashcards from Start here's word list, reread whatever felt shaky, second mock call (a derated truck).

## Week 2: the rest of the truck
1. **Day 8.** Read Cooling system, Oils and fluids, Parts A to Z: Cooling system, Lubrication. Do: say what milky oil and disappearing coolant point to.
2. **Day 9.** Read Fuel system, Electrical, Parts A to Z: Fuel system, Electrical. Do: list the four likely causes of "truck won't start" in order.
3. **Day 10.** Read Drivetrain, Parts A to Z: Clutch and transmission, Driveline and axles. Do: match Eaton, I-Shift and DT12 to their brands.
4. **Day 11.** Read Steering and suspension, Parts A to Z: Steering, Suspension. Do: say what pulling to one side and uneven tire wear point to.
5. **Day 12.** Read Trailers and coupling, Parts A to Z: Frame, fifth wheel and coupling, Trailer, Reefer unit. Do: list what to ask on a reefer alarm call.
6. **Day 13.** Read Cab, AC and body, Pre-trip inspection, Parts A to Z: HVAC and APU, Lights. Do: watch a pre-trip video and name each part before the driver does.
7. **Day 14.** Review. Do: three mock calls back to back (derate, blowout, no start).

## Week 3: planning, money and compliance
1. **Day 15.** Read Preventive maintenance. Do: plan PM A, B and C dates for five made-up units with different mileage.
2. **Day 16.** Read DOT and compliance. Do: explain OOS, the annual inspection and what happens at a roadside stop.
3. **Day 17.** Read Parts and prices, Love's Truck Care and Speedco services. Do: from memory, give a price range for five common jobs, then check.
4. **Day 18.** Read Invoice walkthrough. Do: find the red flags in an old invoice you have handled.
5. **Day 19.** Read the Glossary end to end. Do: flashcards for every word you could not explain.
6. **Day 20.** Reread the cost per mile part of How fleets work and the planning half of Start here. Do: prepare answers for "how would you lower maintenance cost?" and "what do you do when a truck keeps breaking down?"
7. **Day 21.** Final. Do: five mock calls, then write down what you got wrong and reread those sections.

## Every day, 10 minutes
- Ten flashcards from the word list or the glossary.
- One fault code or part looked up in Learn.
