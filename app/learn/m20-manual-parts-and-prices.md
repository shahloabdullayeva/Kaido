# Parts and prices
Rough 2026 US prices, installed (parts plus labor) unless the row says otherwise.

## Overview
Rough 2026 US prices, installed (parts plus labor) unless the row says otherwise. Real prices swing with region, brand, dealer vs independent shop, and whether it's a roadside job. Use these to spot an invoice that is way off, not to argue over small differences. Rows marked **(est.)** are general industry estimates; the rest come from the pages in Sources.
