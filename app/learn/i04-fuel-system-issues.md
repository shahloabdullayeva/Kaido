# Fuel system issues
Gelled fuel, water, air leaks, filters, pumps and injectors.
::: issue Gelled fuel in cold weather | stop
looks: Fuel in the filter bowl or a clear line looks cloudy, waxy or like petroleum jelly. Filter element coated in wax.
feel: Engine loses power, stumbles, then dies; or will not start on a cold morning.
why: Number 2 diesel starts clouding around 10-20 °F. No anti-gel or summer fuel bought in the south then driven north.
do: Warm the truck (heated shop, tarp and heater), add an emergency de-gel product, change fuel filters. Prevent with winter blend and anti-gel at every fill under 20 °F.
cost: Road call and filters $300-800.
:::
::: issue Water in fuel | soon
dash: Water-in-fuel lamp.
looks: Water layer at the bottom of the separator bowl (clear, separate from the yellow diesel), or cloudy fuel.
why: Condensation in half-full tanks, bad fuel stop, rain at a loose tank cap.
do: Drain the separator (valve at the bottom of the bowl) until clean fuel comes out. If it keeps coming back, drain the tanks.
:::
::: issue Air in the fuel system | soon
looks: Bubbles in clear fuel lines, foamy fuel in the filter housing.
feel: Rough running, stalling, hard starting after sitting.
why: Ran out of fuel, loose filter, cracked fuel line, bad O-ring on the filter housing, leaking crossover line.
do: Prime the system. Find the leak if it comes back.
:::
::: issue Fuel filter plugged | soon
dash: Fuel filter restriction lamp or code, low fuel pressure code.
feel: Power loss on hills, surging.
do: Change fuel filters. Normal interval 25,000-50,000 miles depending on engine; sooner with bad fuel.
cost: $80-200 parts, 30-60 minutes.
:::
::: issue Fuel leak at tank, line or filter | soon
looks: Wet, shiny area with dirt stuck to it; drip under the tank; fuel stains running down the tank; strong diesel smell.
why: Tank strap rubbed through the tank, loose fitting, cracked fuel line, filter not tight, crossover line rubbed.
do: Small weep: shop soon. Drip or pour: stop. Fuel leaks are also an out-of-service violation.
dot: Any dripping fuel leak is out of service.
:::
::: issue Tank strap broken or loose | soon
looks: Strap broken, tank shifted on its brackets, rub marks on the tank.
do: Replace strap ($100-300). A loose tank rubs through and leaks.
:::
::: issue Fuel cap missing or wrong | watch
looks: Cap missing, rag stuffed in the neck, or a non-vented cap.
do: Replace cap ($20-60). Dirt and water get in fast.
:::
::: issue Injector failure | soon
feel: Rough idle, misfire, knock, low power.
looks: White or black smoke, fuel in the oil.
dash: Cylinder balance codes, injector circuit codes (Cummins SPN 651-656, Detroit similar).
check: Cylinder cutout test with a laptop.
do: Replace the bad injector or a full set. One injector $500-1,200 installed; a full set $3,000-7,000.
:::
::: issue High-pressure fuel pump or rail pressure fault | soon
dash: SPN 157 low rail pressure, SPN 164 or 5571 relief valve.
feel: Low power, no start, stalling.
do: Shop. Check filters and leaking injectors first. Pump $2,000-5,000 installed.
:::
::: issue Bad fuel (contaminated or wrong fuel) | stop
looks: Gasoline or DEF put in the diesel tank; fuel very dark, smells wrong.
do: Do not start the engine if wrong fuel was found at the pump. Drain and flush. Running gas or DEF through a common-rail system can destroy the pump and injectors ($8,000+).
:::
::: issue Fuel gauge or sender wrong | watch
looks: Gauge stuck, jumps around, or says full when tanks are low. In Kaido the fuel % does not rise after a fill.
why: Failed sending unit, wiring, or tanks not balanced (crossover line plugged so one tank stays full).
do: Sender $150-400 installed. Check the crossover line.
:::
