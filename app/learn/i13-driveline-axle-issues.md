# Driveline and axle issues
Driveshafts, U-joints, carrier bearings, differentials and interaxle dividers.
::: issue U-joint worn | soon
looks: Rust-colored powder around the U-joint caps, missing grease, cap moves when you push the shaft up and down.
hear: Clunk on take-off, squeak at low speed.
feel: Vibration that rises with speed.
do: Replace ($150-400 per joint installed). A U-joint that breaks drops the driveshaft.
:::
::: issue Driveshaft loose or bent | stop
looks: Shaft wobbles, dents, missing balance weights, bolts loose at the yoke.
feel: Strong vibration.
do: Stop. A shaft that falls out can pole-vault the truck or hit the air lines.
:::
::: issue Carrier (center) bearing worn | soon
looks: Rubber mount torn, bearing sagging.
feel: Vibration at take-off and at speed.
do: Replace ($300-700 installed).
:::
::: issue Differential leaking | soon
looks: Gear oil at the pinion seal (front of the axle where the driveshaft connects), at the cover or at the axle shaft flange.
smell: Strong sulfur gear oil smell.
do: Check level, fix seal ($300-800).
:::
::: issue Differential noise | soon
hear: Whine on acceleration or deceleration that changes with road speed; clunks in turns.
why: Worn ring and pinion, bearings, low oil, wrong oil.
do: Check oil and drain plug for metal. Rebuild $3,000-6,000.
:::
::: issue Interaxle (power divider) problem | soon
hear: Grinding, clunks when the interaxle lock is used.
why: Driver locking the divider while wheels spin, worn divider, mismatched tire sizes front to rear drive axle.
do: Shop. Teach drivers to only lock the divider at a stop or at low steady speed.
:::
::: issue Axle shaft broken | stop
looks: Truck will not move though the engine and transmission work, broken shaft visible at the hub flange, flange bolts sheared.
do: Tow or road service.
:::
