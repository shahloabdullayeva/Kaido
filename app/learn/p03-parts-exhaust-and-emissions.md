# Exhaust and emissions
Each part: where it is, what it does, how it fails, and a rough installed price.

## Parts
| Part | Where / what it does | Signs of trouble | Installed (approx) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exhaust manifold | Collects exhaust from the cylinders | Cracks, ticking, exhaust smell in cab | $1,000 to $2,500 |
| Exhaust manifold gaskets and studs | Seal the manifold | Ticking leak, soot marks | $400 to $1,200 |
| Downpipe and exhaust clamps | Turbo to aftertreatment | Leaks, broken clamps | $200 to $800 |
| EGR valve | Lets exhaust back into the intake | Sticking, low power, EGR codes | $800 to $2,000 |
| EGR cooler | Cools recirculated exhaust with coolant | Coolant loss, white smoke | $2,000 to $5,000 |
| EGR pipes and gaskets | Carry exhaust to the intake | Leaks, cracks | $300 to $1,000 |
| Aftertreatment system (ATS) box | The big can under the passenger side holding DOC, DPF and SCR | Codes, derate | See parts below |
| DOC | First stage; burns some pollutants, heats exhaust | Face plugging, regen failures | $1,500 to $4,000 |
| DPF | Traps soot | Needs regens and cleaning; derate when full | Cleaning $300 to $900; new $3,000 to $8,000 |
| DPF differential pressure sensor | Measures how full the DPF is | False full readings, regen codes | $150 to $500 |
| Exhaust temperature sensors | Several along the system | Codes, failed regens | $150 to $400 each |
| Hydrocarbon doser (7th injector) | Sprays fuel into the exhaust for active regens | Clogged, failed regens | $400 to $1,200 |
| SCR catalyst | Where DEF turns NOx into nitrogen and water | Low efficiency codes | $3,000 to $8,000 |
| DEF tank and tank sender | Blue cap tank with level and temperature sender | Wrong level reading, derate | Sender $300 to $900; tank $800 to $2,000 |
| DEF pump | Pushes DEF to the doser | Derate, pump codes | $400 to $1,200 |
| DEF heater and heated lines | Keep DEF from freezing | Winter derates | $200 to $800 |
| DEF doser (injector) | Sprays DEF into the exhaust | Crystals, clogged, derate | $300 to $900 |
| NOx sensors (inlet and outlet) | Measure NOx before and after the SCR | Very common failure, derate | $400 to $1,200 each |
| Muffler / tailpipe | End of the exhaust | Rust, holes, noise | $200 to $800 |
