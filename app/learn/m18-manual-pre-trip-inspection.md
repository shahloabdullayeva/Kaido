# Pre-trip inspection
Before driving, every driver walks around the truck and checks it.

## Overview
Before driving, every driver walks around the truck and checks it. It's required by law, it's on the CDL test, and it's how most problems get caught early.

## The three codes that cover almost everything

- **PMS** = Properly Mounted and Secure. Said about anything bolted on, which is nearly everything.
- **CBB** = not Cracked, Bent or Broken. For metal and hard parts.
- **ABC** = no Abrasions, Bulges or Cuts. For anything rubber: hoses, tires, air bags.
- Plus: if fluid or air runs through it, **not leaking**.

## The sections, in order

| Section | What gets checked |
|---|---|
| Engine compartment | Leaks under the truck; belts (alternator, water pump) with 1/2" to 3/4" of play; hoses; coolant, oil and power steering levels; air compressor; steering gear and linkage (pitman arm, drag link, tie rods, castle nuts and cotter pins); front suspension (spring hangers, leaf springs, U bolts, shocks); front brakes (hose, chamber, slack adjuster and pushrod, drum, lining); front wheel (rim, lug nuts, hub seal, tire) |
| Driver door and fuel area | Mirrors, door, steps, fuel tank and cap, battery box, driveshaft, exhaust, frame, catwalk |
| Coupling system | Air lines and gladhands, 7 way cord, fifth wheel (mounting, locking jaws around the kingpin, release arm locked), apron and gap, platform |
| Trailer | Doors and hinges, lights and reflectors, trailer suspension (it varies, so the guide asks you to note which type), brakes, wheels, tires, landing gear, tandem slider and locking pins, mud flaps |
| Light check | Headlights high and low, turn signals, four way flashers, clearance and marker lights, brake lights |
| In cab and brake tests | Gauges, horn, wipers, heater and defroster, mirrors, seat belt, emergency kit (fire extinguisher, reflective triangles, spare fuses); then the air brake tests from the Air brakes section |

**Why it matters to the maintenance side.** Every defect the driver finds goes on a **DVIR** (Driver Vehicle Inspection Report). Maintenance must fix or confirm it before the truck goes out again. A driver who writes "slack adjuster out of adjustment" or "marker light out" is creating a work order for the maintenance team.
