Getting the file from eManager

  1. Sign in to eManager at efsllc.com → Customer Logins → EFS eManager.
  2. Select Program → Reports/Exports → Transaction Report.
  3. Pick the date range and choose CSV as the format. Include unit, driver and odometer if the report offers them.
  4. Upload it here.

You can also set the report up as a scheduled report emailed to you every day or week in eManager, then upload each one here.

About "Data Sharing Preferences": that screen shares your transactions with a partner system. EFS processes the request (up to five business days) and then gives you a Data Feed Username and Password, which are different from your eManager login. If Kaido or "other" cannot be picked as the partner, ask your EFS account manager for a transaction data feed for your own fleet software. Once you have the data feed username and password, Kaido can be set up to pull purchases on its own, and this upload becomes the backup.
